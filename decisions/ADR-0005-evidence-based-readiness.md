# ADR-0005 — Evidence-based readiness and recommendations

Status: Draft pending merge
Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision
Readiness will use an explainable evidence-based rules engine rather than clicks, a simplistic fixed percentage, or an opaque statistical model.

## Evidence families
- recall
- understanding/application
- exam performance
- full simulation where relevant

## Rules
- Readiness and evidence confidence are separate concepts.
- Precise readiness must not be shown before minimum evidence requirements are met.
- Recent evidence generally carries more weight than stale evidence.
- Recommendations must trace back to identifiable evidence gaps.
- The model must remain testable and explainable in simple learner-friendly language.
