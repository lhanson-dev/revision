# ADR-0021 — Foundation Course Truth semantic seed

**Status:** Proposed for Founder approval with the implementing PR  
**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Decision owner:** Founder / Content Factory  
**Authority:** `80-company-workflows/Content Factory Foundation and Asset Production Model.md`; `40-evidence-and-trust/Educational Content Source Licensing and Provenance Standard.md`

## Context

The first v2 atomic Foundation proof correctly created one canonical Course Truth node for every governed `skillsOrKnowledge` item, but the initial Course Truth worker was still supplied mostly topic labels. Its contract simultaneously prohibited unsupported facts from model memory. The fresh independent reviewer therefore found that the initial Course Knowledge Model was an index of labels rather than sufficiently teachable/generative subject truth.

The same proof showed why repeated remediation is the wrong ownership boundary. Cycle 1 had to manufacture definitions, methods, formulae, interpretation boundaries, application contexts and misconceptions that should have existed in the governed compilation input. Later reviews then spent cycles correcting formula precision and scope that could have been deterministic upstream evidence.

## Decision

For the Foundation-native AQA A-level Business 7132 / 2027 proof profile, the governed Revision Course Truth seed becomes a **Revision-owned semantic evidence seed** rather than a list of topic labels.

Each atomic `skillsOrKnowledge` item must state enough candidate subject semantics to bound generation, including as applicable:

- the concept/skill distinction and intended meaning;
- relationships or decision boundaries;
- exact formula/method rules where the seed governs a quantitative method;
- interpretation limits and common misuse boundaries; and
- the explicit allowed method set where a broad label could otherwise invite model expansion.

The seed remains `REVISION_OWNED` candidate evidence with retained OPEN upstream evidence references. It is not qualified-human approval and does not become learner content directly.

The Foundation Course Truth worker must continue to use only the governed seed scope and must not broaden named formula, ratio or method sets from model memory. A material seed change changes the retained source fingerprint and requires a fresh Foundation Candidate.

## Rights and provenance boundary

AQA remains `REFERENCE_ONLY` and contributes controlled Board Alignment facts only. This decision does not permit AQA source prose to enter generative context.

The semantic seed retains the existing OPEN DfE and LibreTexts upstream evidence references and remains subject to live source/licence preflight. Revision authorship of the candidate semantic statements does not remove the later independent-review and qualified-expert assurance obligations.

## Assurance consequence

The AQA proof seed must retain all 82 existing canonical atomic knowledge/skill obligations while preventing a regression to label-only entries. High-risk quantitative methods receive explicit deterministic regression assertions, including grouped percentage-change logic and bounded financial-ratio scope.

A clean compiler/test result is not Foundation approval. The newly compiled Foundation must still pass deterministic assurance, fresh-context independent review, and Slice 3C qualified subject/assessment review before `foundation_approved`.

## Deliberately excluded

This ADR does not:

- raise the three-cycle remediation limit;
- weaken independent review;
- treat AI review as qualified-human calibration;
- authorise learner-asset generation;
- resolve the separate Paper 2 / Paper 3 pre-calibration assembly-boundary finding; or
- claim that the semantic seed is permanently complete for future cohorts or specifications.

The assessment-assembly boundary is a separate short governed hardening slice and must be released before another paid Slice 3B proof is justified.

## Consequences

- The next AQA Foundation compile must produce a new source-set/Foundation fingerprint.
- Historical v1/v2 retained candidates remain valid historical evidence and are not rewritten.
- Course Truth generation should spend less remediation capacity inventing foundational semantics.
- Future subject profiles should adopt the same evidence principle, but their semantic content and source provenance remain course-specific rather than copied from this Business seed.
