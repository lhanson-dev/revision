# Course Content and Assessment Component Placement

**Status:** Founder-approved product authority  
**Approved:** 2026-08-18  
**Purpose:** Define where shared syllabus learning and paper/component-specific exam preparation belong in the learner hierarchy.

## Governing principle

Revision must model the academic structure the learner actually needs rather than duplicating content because the implementation stores one pack per exam paper or component.

Where an awarding body allows the same syllabus content to be assessed across multiple papers or components, that shared content belongs to the **course/specification level**.

The learner should learn a topic once, practise it once, and build one coherent evidence picture for that course. The existence of several exam papers must not create several apparent copies of the same syllabus.

## Default learner hierarchy

For a course whose papers assess shared content, the learner-facing hierarchy is:

```text
Subject
└── Course / specification
    ├── Overview
    ├── Learn
    │   └── Shared course topics
    ├── Practice
    │   └── Shared recall, application and quantitative practice
    ├── Exam Prep
    │   ├── Shared exam technique where genuinely common
    │   └── Paper / component
    │       ├── Paper-specific question practice
    │       └── Timed / full simulation
    └── Progress
        ├── Shared course/topic evidence
        └── Paper-specific exam evidence where useful
```

## Shared-content rule

Learn, general Practice and course/topic Progress should normally appear once at course/specification level when the official specification makes the same content assessable across multiple papers/components.

This includes, where supported by the content model:

- topic explanations and notes;
- flashcards and recall;
- general quick checks;
- cross-topic links;
- general case/application practice;
- formulas and quantitative drills; and
- course-level topic evidence and recommendations.

Revision must not multiply topic counts, apparent coverage gaps or REV recommendations merely because the same topic can appear on several papers.

## Exam Prep rule

Paper/component distinctions belong inside Exam Prep when their main educational purpose is assessment format rather than syllabus ownership.

Paper/component-specific Exam Prep may include:

- official paper structure and timing;
- command-word or response-format guidance that genuinely differs by paper;
- targeted written exam questions;
- source/data/case handling specific to that paper;
- timed sections; and
- full-paper/component simulations.

A learner should be able to choose the paper they want to prepare for without first navigating through duplicate copies of the shared syllabus.

## Distinct-content exception

Some qualifications genuinely assign different content, themes, texts, skills or specification areas to different papers/components.

In those cases, the component may own its own Learn, Practice and Progress context.

The decision must be based on current awarding-body authority, not on a generic assumption that every numbered paper either does or does not own distinct content.

## Evidence and REV

Where learning content is shared across papers/components:

- course/topic learning evidence should form one course-level evidence picture;
- paper-specific exam attempts may retain their paper/component identity;
- REV should not interpret identical course topics attached to several paper packs as separate weaknesses or missing coverage;
- global progress should count the shared syllabus once; and
- paper-specific readiness claims must remain distinguishable from broader course/topic evidence where the evidence model supports that distinction.

Internal storage may retain paper/module identifiers for compatibility, provenance or exam attempts. Internal identifiers do not determine the learner-facing academic hierarchy.

## Current Business application

### AQA A-level Business 7132

All three papers can assess the full course content. Revision therefore presents the ten shared course areas once at AQA A-level Business course level. Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 sit inside Exam Prep for their distinct formats and simulations.

### AQA AS Business 7131

Both papers can assess all six AS content areas. Revision therefore presents those six areas once at AQA AS Business course level. Paper-specific exam preparation belongs inside Exam Prep.

The current repository contains an assured AS Paper 2 pack but does not yet contain an assured AS Paper 1 pack. The learner experience must show only paper-specific material that actually exists; it must not invent or imply publication of Paper 1 content.

## Future-subject rule

This authority applies generically to Business, Spanish and future qualifications.

When adding a course, content production and implementation must determine from the official specification whether learning is:

1. course-wide/shared across assessment components; or
2. genuinely component-specific.

That determination must be reflected in the learner hierarchy and assurance evidence before publication.
