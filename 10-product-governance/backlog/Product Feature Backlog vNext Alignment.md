## FI-018 — Teacher / School Insight

**Proposed lifecycle state:** To Do  
**Prior state:** Parked  
**Founder direction:** 24 August 2026

The Founder has now explicitly established that a Teacher and School route belongs in Revision as a future independent product/commercial workstream, while preserving the Student-first product and the boundary against becoming a general-purpose LMS.

The intended direction is:

- Teacher Free proves the insight proposition with one active class;
- Teacher Paid unlocks multiple classes and on-demand REV analysis of structured Student/class evidence;
- Teacher Premium must add a qualitatively different capability, with proactive REV teaching intelligence as the current direction;
- the Teacher product exists to help Teachers understand strengths, weaknesses and teaching/intervention priorities, not primarily to assign Revision work;
- School is an organisation and institutional payer route, not a fourth user type;
- Teacher/School payment must not automatically grant unlimited Paid/Premium Student capability;
- Teacher/School workflows must not require Parents to purchase consumer subscriptions; and
- exact Teacher/School pricing, class allowances, REV allowances, privacy boundaries, School organisation model and implementation remain Definition-of-Ready work.

This direction is approved for product backlog progression from `Parked` to `To Do`; it is not implementation approval and does not satisfy the `Analyse → Ready` gate.

## FI-022 — Parent Experience and Linking

**Proposed lifecycle state:** To Do  
**Captured:** 24 August 2026

The Founder has explicitly established that a separate Parent experience belongs in Revision.

The intended direction is:

- Parent is a user type linked to a Student;
- Parent and Student account creation/linking must support Student-led and Parent-led invitation routes;
- a Parent invitation must not bypass the Student's own account creation and canonical Student onboarding;
- a linked Parent should have a genuinely useful Free view;
- a Parent normally funds a stronger Student subscription rather than buying a separate standalone Parent subscription;
- the linked Parent experience may unlock clearly named additional capabilities according to the Student tier;
- payment and Parent access are distinct: payment alone does not create access to Student data;
- Parent access must remain bounded and must not expose private REV conversations, private notes, raw Student work or detailed surveillance by default; and
- exact Free/Paid/Premium Parent capabilities, relationship verification, legal/privacy controls, measurement and implementation remain Definition-of-Ready work.

This direction is approved for `To Do`; it is not implementation approval.

## Sequencing protection

FI-018, FI-022 and FI-002 must not block agreement, implementation or testing of the initial Student solution. The core Student proposition must remain testable end-to-end on Free with no Parent, Teacher, School or billing dependency.
