## Why another bounded Ready decision is necessary

FI-001's existing Founder-approved programme-first/R2 scope is in production. PRs #702 and #709 now present genuine available materials in the next 14 days; an actual user confirmed the once-empty forecast is populated. **But planning more one-time topic activities is not a durable learning system.** Current scored evidence is largely topic-level; specification skill identities do not persist reliably; there is no complete **assessed weakness → repair → independent new recheck → new shared plan** loop and no honest per-paper remaining workload-versus-capacity analysis.

The currently approved R2 Ready explicitly excluded R3 strategic optimisation and standalone FI-009/010/011/012 capabilities. The canonical backlog still marks FI-009/010/011/012 **New**. This proposed change deliberately resolves that governance boundary rather than inferring approval from the Founder's request to continue. Only the minimum **existing-authored-content** FI-009/010/011 subset necessary for the connected learning loop is proposed; **FI-012 remains New** and AI marking is excluded.

## One student outcome and deliverable boundaries

A real signed-in learner with enrolled AQA Business 7132 and Psychology 7182, correct selected Paper 3 options, actual exam dates and 45 minutes across five weekdays should see:

1. The **exact applicable skill and course/paper demand** associated with a valid lesson and assessment opportunity, and an honest `unknown`/unsupported state when mapping, content or marking quality is insufficient.
2. A saved, independently scored answer with question/content/version and marking provenance that can support or challenge **that exact skill**, not every skill in its topic. Self-rated cards, unscored Learn and self-assessed written work have weaker or separate evidence meaning.
3. A supported, differentiated next treatment: appropriate Learn/repair or *different* independently assessable prompt, not the same coached answer; real improvement or persistent weakness changes evidence-driven priorities, without a new mastery formula.
4. A single programme through the last known exam, realistic capacity and honest **source-backed workload bounds plus unquantifiable gaps** for every paper. The detailed executable plan still rolls for 14 days; later weeks are provisional, not dated task debts. Insufficient time is explained calmly and prioritised among supported, applicable interventions.
5. Consistent Home/Plan/REV/Progress, safe reload/resume, owner-only data and real learner observation before declaring this outcome delivered.

**Delivery slices:** B1 #704 exact skill evidence/versioned persistence; B2 #705 existing-asset repair/independent retest and minimal FI-009/010/011; B3 FI-001 R3 paper-scoped workload/capacity and shortfall; B4 authenticated end-to-end acceptance and content gap handoff #698. Slice merges are individually Founder-approved and may release early for family testing **without falsely closing the parent outcome**. No new planner, self-contained practice engine, universal cadence, LLM question generator, adaptive-decay claim, grade prediction, arbitrary daily quotas, new subscription barrier, unnecessary account flags or unvalidated written marking.
