# Mission, Vision and Purpose

**Status:** Draft authority candidate — v0.1  
**Purpose:** Define why Revision exists, what it is building now, and the future it is aiming towards.

## Purpose

Revision exists to help students achieve the grades they are capable of by making revision clearer, more personalised and less stressful.

It should help students feel in control of their learning, understand what they need to do, access personalised support and approach exams with confidence grounded in real preparation.

Better exam outcomes are the ultimate objective. Feeling in control, reducing unnecessary stress, building confidence, strengthening understanding and improving revision habits are important because they help students perform closer to their potential.

## Mission

We are building a product that makes students feel in control of their revision.

Revision should turn a large and potentially overwhelming body of material into a clear, personalised path, helping students understand what to work on, what they have already mastered and what needs attention next.

## Vision

Every student can have a personalised study coach that understands their curriculum, knows where they need help and guides them through exam preparation.

That coach should combine reliable educational content, evidence about the student's progress and proactive guidance so that high-quality personalised revision support is available at scale.

## Success hierarchy

When outcomes compete, Revision should optimise broadly in this order:

1. Better exam results
2. Feeling in control
3. Lower stress
4. Confidence
5. Stronger understanding
6. Better revision habits

This hierarchy does not mean lower-ranked outcomes are unimportant. It clarifies that Revision is ultimately an exam-preparation product: supporting understanding, habits, confidence and wellbeing should contribute to stronger preparation and performance rather than becoming detached goals.

## Scope of ambition

Revision should first prove that it can create meaningful value for a real student. The foundations should nevertheless support growth into a serious UK revision platform and, if the evidence justifies it, a broader education product.
