# Strategic Positioning

**Status:** Draft authority candidate — v0.2  
**Purpose:** Define what Revision is, how it should be understood, who it serves first, and what it should not become.

## Primary position

Revision is a personalised revision system presented to the student as a proactive AI tutor and study coach.

The student-facing idea is an AI tutor that knows the relevant subjects, understands the student's progress and needs, and supports them through revision towards the results they are capable of achieving.

The underlying system must be more than a conversational interface. It should use curriculum knowledge, progress evidence, revision activity and exam preparation data to guide the student intelligently over time.

Revision's strategic category is **Personalised Revision Intelligence**: it should know what the learner is studying, learn what the learner knows, work out what matters next and help the learner improve it.

## Core differentiation

Revision should differentiate through personal support and guidance based on an individual student's progress and needs.

The product should not simply provide the same resources to every learner. It should increasingly understand:

- what the student is studying;
- what they have covered;
- what they appear to understand;
- where they are weaker;
- what they may be at risk of forgetting;
- what exam-technique patterns are emerging;
- what they should work on next; and
- how best to support them towards their exams.

The value is the combination of curriculum-aware knowledge and an ongoing understanding of the student.

Revision should become more useful as the learner uses it. Useful learning interactions should improve the structured learner model where evidence and product authority permit, so later teaching, practice, feedback and recommendations can become more relevant.

## Student proposition

A simple explanation of Revision should be:

> We know your subjects and we get to know you. Revision supports and guides you through your revision so you can get the results you are capable of.

A stronger strategic shorthand is:

> **Revision knows what you're studying, learns what you know, works out what matters next, and helps you improve it.**

These are directional propositions rather than final marketing copy.

## Experience ambition

Revision should feel engaging, personal and relevant to a teenager rather than like another school system.

It must avoid the sterile, institutional or administrative feel common to school platforms. Educational rigour should sit underneath an experience that students actually want to use.

The student should experience a simple connected loop rather than a collection of unrelated tools: recommendation → learning → practice → feedback → improvement → better future recommendation.

## What Revision is not

Revision should not become:

- merely a content library;
- a generic AI chatbot with no persistent understanding of the student or curriculum;
- a toolbox of disconnected AI study utilities;
- a school learning-management system;
- a conventional homework platform;
- a replacement for teachers; or
- a product whose engagement mechanics matter more than useful learning and exam preparation.

The strongest red line is becoming a boring school platform that students disengage from.

## Initial market

The first market is UK students preparing for GCSE and A-level qualifications, initially through a direct-to-consumer product.

The first proof point is usefulness for real students such as Jamie. The architecture and product model should nevertheless allow the proposition to scale across subjects, qualifications and a broader UK market if validated.

## Position relative to generic AI

Revision should earn its place alongside general-purpose AI by knowing both the student's curriculum and the student's learning journey.

A generic assistant can answer an isolated question. Revision should be able to place that question in context: what the student is studying, what they have already demonstrated, what they still need to cover, what they repeatedly misunderstand, what evidence may have become stale, what matters for their exam and what they should do next.

That persistent, curriculum-aware guidance is intended to be the core advantage.

REV should therefore be understood as the conversational and proactive interface to a wider learner-intelligence system, not as the product's only source of intelligence.

## Positioning test

A proposed capability strengthens Revision when it improves at least one of the following without undermining the others:

1. knowledge of the relevant curriculum;
2. reliable knowledge of the individual student;
3. quality of personalised guidance;
4. ability to help the student learn, repair weaknesses and retain knowledge;
5. ability to keep the student calm, engaged and on track; or
6. ability to improve preparation for exam outcomes.

Features that merely add content, AI interactions, competitor parity or novelty without strengthening the personalised revision system should be challenged.
