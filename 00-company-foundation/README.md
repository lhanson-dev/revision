# Company Foundation

This folder contains Revision's approved Founder and company-level authority.

## Current authority

- `Founder Doctrine.md` — founding beliefs, strategic intent, educational integrity and decision test.
- `Mission Vision and Purpose.md` — why Revision exists, what it is building and the future it is aiming towards.
- `Strategic Positioning.md` — primary market position, differentiation, initial market and what Revision should not become.

## Future authority

This folder may also contain company values, business operating model, strategic red lines and other Founder-level authority where required.
