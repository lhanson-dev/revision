# Brand and Experience

This folder contains normative authority for Revision's learner experience, language and visual brand system.

## Active sources

- `Learner Design System.md` — **start here for learner visual and interaction design**. Canonical learner foundations, responsive geometry, REV visual presence, themes, forms, system states, learning composition and design-quality gate.
- `Product UX Principles.md` — learner experience and journey-quality principles.
- `Tone of Voice Framework.md` — learner-facing language and interaction tone.
- `Visual Brand System.md` — company-wide identity and wider cross-channel brand expression. For the learner product, overlapping visual/interaction rules are superseded by `Learner Design System.md`.
- `Interactive Component Quality Standard.md` — specialist action/control quality contract.
- `REV Guidance and Conversation Pattern.md` — specialist REV behaviour, recommendation/conversation pattern and voice.
- `Educational Treatment System.md` — specialist educational-treatment meaning/anatomy.
- `Subject Accent Colour System.md` — subject hue/mark mapping and subject-identity usage.
- `Identity Asset Usage Rules.md` — exact wordmark/Living E asset geometry and safe use.

## Candidate / supporting authority

- `Emotional Experience Principles.md` — desired emotional state and motivational principles; currently a draft authority candidate.

## Knowledge rule

The numbered authority folder contains normative truth about what Revision should do. For learner design, `Learner Design System.md` is the canonical entry point and specialist authorities are read by reference rather than restating its rules. Visual explorations and boards in `research/brand-studio/` are evidence/reference only until deliberately promoted through the governed approval path.

Implementation evidence lives in code and `docs/technical/`; it does not override this authority.
