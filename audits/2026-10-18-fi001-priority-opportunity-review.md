## Acceptance rule

The mandatory [FI-001 Student-Value Scenarios](../10-product-governance/backlog/FI-001%20Programme%20Value%20Scenarios.md) require a real exam-backwards strategy, not a list of feasible tasks. A scenario cannot receive educational PASS merely because it has a programme, a paper-linked question, an intact Start route, or zero capacity violations. The intended allocation among prerequisite learning, retrieval, gap repair, specific paper-answer technique and competing courses needs qualified judgement for the particular student evidence and time. This document supplies traceable counterfactual observations, **not** a claim that the allocations are optimal or pedagogically validated.

If exam practice does not fit, is the stated reason exact and is a feasible alternative selected? No arbitrary mandatory essay or invented grade target.
2. **SV-12:** When capacity changes from zero to a single recurring work opportunity, is prioritising introductory explanation over exam-style opportunities defensible given the observed evidence and paper dates? Are omissions and course-share trade-offs comprehensible in Month, Week and REV?
3. **SV-03:** In the final days before Psychology's paper, the model allocates two Learn activities and no response-section practice. Is that appropriate given sparse mixed evidence and exam-format demands? What would a qualified teacher recommend instead, and why?

After genuine scored work, missed time or moved dates, do Month, Week, Day, Home and REV provide a coherent same-decision story?

## E-13: Same-week prerequisite may be scheduled in reverse calendar order (18 October 2026)

**Source-level defect found in educational review:** The programme prevented a dependent `check` while its `learn` candidate remained outstanding but did not enforce the dates after selecting the Learn. With a Friday 10-minute and Saturday 20-minute opportunity, a 20-minute Learn can correctly land on Saturday while its 10-minute dependent check incorrectly lands on Friday, because the predecessor is no longer in the outstanding map. The student's timetable thus contradicts its own required learn-before-check treatment despite satisfying the weekly sum.

**Bounded draft correction:** in `src/engine/planning/preparation.ts`, when a prerequisite has been allocated in the current week, the dependent activity may use only a day on/after that *actual* predecessor forecast date (same-day ordering remains guaranteed by `buildAdaptivePlan`); if no later eligible whole slot exists, it must remain unallocated. Add a deterministic regression with both a feasible Sunday-after-Saturday check and no later slot, including reverse-order daily capacity inputs. There is no change to educational thresholds, arbitrary revisit intervals, real learner evidence or source content. Exact-head validation is outstanding. This is a genuine hard sequencing defect, not evidence that the educational benefit of the whole plan has been accepted.

## E-15: Boundaries of accepted-session reads must not produce phantom available time (19 October 2026)

**Found in SV-07 / scarce-time technical review:** The existing accepted-session query is bounded, but `focusMinutesAfterKnownCommitments` and planner-model's per-paper/day callbacks keyed their decision to the *entire week's* final date. If the week straddled `acceptedLoadedThrough`, an actual accepted Friday commitment was ignored because Saturday/Sunday lay outside the query. An unknown near accepted-session read could be mistaken for spare capacity. The aggregate subtraction also erased unrelated available weekdays if a saved day was overbooked. This conflicts with the product's hard daily-capacity, accepted choice and truthful-unknown rules.

**Bounded candidate repair:** account for booking minutes day-by-day only where the date is covered by the loaded accepted-session records, clamp each day to zero separately and preserve future unqueried dates as provisional. For failed reads intersecting the queried days, show unknown rather than inventing free time. Unit assurance for a partial boundary week, overbooked day, failed read and a real modelled session/travel through the bridge is committed in PR #637. No widened accepted-session query, no added persistence, no changed evidence policy and no additional time invented. Exact-head CI and five-view acceptance are still required. Educational HOLD remains in force.

## E-18: Unjustified knowledge-check prerequisite blocked valid exam-response opportunity (19 October 2026)

## E-19: A one-mark exam question was incorrectly budgeted as 30 minutes (19 October 2026)

The controlled `SV-06` 20-minute weekly opportunity and `SV-12` 30-minute opportunity revealed an **activity-size misrepresentation**: `ExamSimulator` focused Start opens one authored question, but the programme attributed 30 minutes to it without using actual published question marks or paper duration. Thus a valid one-mark multiple-choice item became impossible in a 20-minute slot and a 25-mark extended essay was given the same estimate. The absence of exam-style allocations under 20 minutes was partly a scheduling-artifact consequence, not solely proof of sensible learning-first prioritisation.

Draft correction introduces one shared deterministic helper in `planner-model.ts`: take the exact, available module/exam/question and allocate the question's proportional duration at its published attempted marks, rounded up to a five-minute scheduling unit (at least five and no more than the original full-paper time); retain the old 30-minute fallback for a target that cannot be verified. For the original Revision-owned Paper 1: 120 minutes and 100 attempted marks → one mark fits 5 minutes and a 25-mark essay remains 30 minutes. It does **not** shorten a full timed paper, change the question or marking scheme, manufacture a capability or suggest that one MCQ completes the exam requirement. The five-minute minimum is an explicit operational hypothesis for educational/usability review, not a scientifically verified time rule. Both direct exact-question recommendation and programme response-option generation are covered. Exact-head comparative regression and user-facing priority adjudication still outstanding.

**Acceptance:** engineering CI and same-input scenario comparison must pass; review whether scarce-time allocations now select meaningful evidence-building opportunities instead of overfilling with one-mark MCQs, and whether real learners perceive estimates as feasible. No educational effectiveness, full AO competence or published content accuracy verdict is inferred. Existing dated evidence retains historical truth.
