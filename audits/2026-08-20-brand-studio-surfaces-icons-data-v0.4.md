## Proposal summary

The v0.4 proposal defines ten named surface families, a rounded line icon language, restrained Living-E-derived graphic motifs, small subject-recognition accents rather than full themes, evidence-first chart selection rules and a derived five-colour data-series palette.

The data proposal deliberately preserves the distinction between coverage, understanding/mastery evidence, exam readiness and plan progress. It also preserves the requirement that material progress judgements explain meaning/evidence rather than presenting unexplained decorative scores.

## Accessibility / evidence findings

- Primary Teal remains too low-contrast against white for a thin essential chart line; Data Teal `#168C7C` is used instead for essential graphical objects.
- Multi-series charts require non-colour differentiation through labels, markers, line styles or patterns.
- Subject colours must never reuse semantic success/warning/error meaning or imply attainment.

## Founder decision — 20 August 2026

The Founder explicitly approved the **Brand Studio v0.4 direction**.

The approval confirms:

- the ten named surface families and their intended jobs;
- the rounded-line icon language and size/stroke rules;
- restrained Living-E-derived graphic motifs;
- subject accents as recognition cues without a fixed subject-to-colour mapping;
- evidence-first chart selection;
- the derived data-series palette `#168C7C`, `#5F7FA3`, `#5F8F73`, `#A5824A`, `#607074`; and
- strict visual separation of coverage, understanding/mastery evidence, exam readiness and plan progress.

The approval does not freeze individual future compositions. Recurring rules are promoted into the draft Brand System in the same governed PR.
