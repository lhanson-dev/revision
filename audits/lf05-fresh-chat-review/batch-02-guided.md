## BUS-LF05-03-16 — Mira Sports Drinks: product or price?

**Question:** Evaluate whether Mira Sports Drinks should introduce a sugar-free premium product rather than cut prices on its current range.

3. Case application: Use Mira's 12% share, 8% sales-volume fall after a £1 price rise, interest in sugar-free products among 80 urban athletes, and cheaper rival offers. The survey is limited and may not represent the wider market.

4. Developed analysis: Explain plausible benefits of premium sugar-free differentiation versus reducing prices to defend sales volumes and share; connect customer demand, margins and brand position, using conditional causal reasoning.

5. Evidence boundaries: The stimulus does not supply contribution per unit, production capacity, launch cost or a reliable price elasticity estimate. Discuss these as unknown decision risks, not established facts or calculable certainties.

6. Reasoned judgement: Compare both choices and justify a conditional recommendation linked to contribution, representativeness of market testing and competitor response. Credit defensible alternatives on the same evidence.

7. Response quality: Strong answers use multiple real case facts, reason through competing consequences and support a qualified judgement; a generic list of four Ps or unsupported claims about profitability remains limited.
