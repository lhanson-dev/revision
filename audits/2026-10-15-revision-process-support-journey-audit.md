## 1. Executive finding

Revision has credible foundations for useful first activities, first-use interruption/recovery, evidence-grounded Home recommendations, exact Start and programme horizons. But **the product cannot yet demonstrate the wider promise of helping a student overcome the ordinary process of revision**. In particular, the current REV conversation has narrow, rule-classified intents and cannot coach a learner through "I cannot start" or "I have only ten minutes" with a real appropriately sized alternative. Nor does a distant weekly topic outlook prove an exam-backwards optimised study programme, or a checkpoint description prove real full-course reassessment.

The important distinction is between a working *path into revision* and a demonstrably supportive *path through difficult revision days*. Don't redesign working flows in order to remedy missing coaching behaviour. Do not declare these outcomes achieved because navigation or screenshots pass.

The Founder's intention is inclusive-by-default process support for **all** students, including students who struggle with task initiation or organisation. Do not add ADHD inference, diagnosis questions, condition labels or medical efficacy claims. Specific accessibility choices require research and representative learner observation.

## 4. Most important verified blocker — REV currently has no ordinary-process coaching route

The current `PlannerRevScreen.tsx` sends free text through `screenForSafeguarding`, then `classifyQuestion` for exactly three categories, plus a named-course preference change. Otherwise it sends `CANNOT_ANSWER_YET` from `rev-answers.ts`. The message says it can answer today's plan, progress, exams and preference changes, with other replies coming later. `tests/e2e/ask-rev.spec.ts` intentionally checks this limitation.

The source therefore **cannot be counted as implementing** the proposed "manageable short start when overwhelmed" REV experience. The already approved `REV Guidance and Conversation Pattern.md` provides voice and boundaries, and the backlog maps richer supported behaviour to FI-003, status **To Do**. The right next action is **Analyse FI-003's bounded task-initiation coaching path through the 17-point Definition of Ready**, rather than sneaking in a new conversational workflow under a documentation or presentation PR.

Target behavioural contract for analysis: learner explicitly requests a smaller start → REV acknowledges briefly → chooses only a genuinely supported shorter activity if one exists, otherwise explains the limitation and offers a truthful supported alternative/break → Start opens the real exact activity → completion creates only valid evidence → planner remains single authority; any material adjustment to preferences requires confirmation. Test ordinary difficulty, significant safeguarding risk and ambiguous phrasing separately. No invented five-minute session, psychological inference, persistent diagnosis or separate recommendation engine.

## 6. Practical test fixtures for the next governed work

1. **Overwhelmed first use:** exact course added; dates unknown; starting check skipped; first real activity still directly reachable; time-to-first-value and hesitation observed.
2. **Ten-minute request:** established learner with 30-minute candidate, legitimate separate shorter content if supported; no retiming fiction; explicit fallback when nothing fits.
3. **Missed week:** real persisted ordinary evidence and accepted commitments, then a seven-day gap and nearer exam; Home/Plan/REV agree on a fresh valid task; no overdue debt or fabricated completion.
4. **Low/zero capacity:** two supported courses and real approaching exams; zero minutes means no pretend allocation; positive limited time shows honest prioritisation and a way to revise voluntarily if safe.
5. **Broader knowledge check:** uncertain breadth and old/contradictory evidence, then representative assessed material; no inflated mastery, right scoped follow-on action.
6. **Ordinary distress vs significant safeguarding:** "I can't start revision" and "I'm overwhelmed by this topic" receive suitable educational guidance; credible harmful-risk language retains the governed safeguarding route.
7. **Actual feedback loop:** wrong scored Practice response, explanation, targeted improvement or valid fallback and evidence change; no passive activity promoted to exam readiness.

These are **proposed acceptance oracles**, not tests that ran during this audit.
