## Evidence basis

The boards deliberately start from approved/current Revision evidence rather than an unrelated redesign:

- approved source colours from the active Visual Brand System;
- Inter/system-sans typography from the current implementation;
- the current learner shell's indigo action language;
- Bright Lime as selective momentum treatment;
- the current REV indigo/cyan orb and glow family;
- current soft-indigo surfaces and restrained shadows; and
- the existing design direction of focused energy, strong hierarchy and rounded surfaces.

Where a treatment goes beyond current approved authority it is labelled proposed, candidate or Experimental.

## v0.1 recommended direction

### Keep

- Deep Ink as primary structural/text colour.
- Revision Indigo as the default branded interaction colour.
- Bright Lime as a deliberately scarce momentum/highest-priority accent rather than a general button colour.
- Canvas / soft-indigo / white as the principal neutral surface family.
- REV's indigo core with cyan glow/orb effects as a distinctive AI identity direction.
- modern heavy sans-serif display hierarchy with highly readable body typography.
- rounded geometry and restrained depth.

### Simplify

The current implementation contains too many unrelated radius values. v0.1 proposes testing a small family:

- `14px` — controls;
- `20px` — standard cards/surfaces;
- `32px` — feature/hero surfaces; and
- pill/circular — status and intentionally rounded identity treatments.

This is a proposed simplification, not yet approved authority.

### Separate semantic and expressive colour

The board treats routine UI colours as semantic roles and keeps more expressive colour for REV, illustration, social, video and feature art direction.

REV cyan (`#7BF3FF` family) is shown as an **Experimental expressive accent**, not as a general product colour.

### Preserve channel differences

- learner product — energetic, calm, task-first;
- marketing/editorial — bolder, larger and more story-led;
- Admin — denser and quieter with shared controls/status semantics;
- social — stronger crop, typography and repeatable motif use; and
- video — stronger motion/title treatment while retaining the same identity.

The examples intentionally do not share one universal card layout.

## Founder confirmation queue

The v0.1 boards are ready for feedback on these decisions:

1. Revision Indigo as the default branded action colour.
2. Bright Lime as a selective momentum/highest-priority CTA accent only.
3. Inter/system-sans as the baseline family, with much larger expressive display use outside dense learning/operations contexts.
4. The proposed `14 / 20 / 32 / pill` radius family.
5. REV cyan as an expressive accent restricted to REV/AI and related art direction unless separately approved.
6. The principle that marketing/social/video may be substantially more expressive than learner/Admin while retaining the same foundations.
7. The current `Revision✦` wordmark should be treated as implementation evidence only and receive a dedicated identity/lock-up review rather than being silently promoted as final brand identity.

## Explicit non-approvals

No approval should be inferred yet for:

- final logo/wordmark lock-ups;
- clear-space/minimum-size rules;
- complete expressive palette;
- subject accent colours;
- final success/warning/error palette;
- exact full type scale;
- final social templates;
- final video transitions or animation timings;
- production CSS/token/component changes; or
- Brand Studio production implementation.

## Recommended next confirmation tranche

After v0.1 feedback, prepare dedicated visual boards for:

1. identity / wordmark / REV lock-ups;
2. expressive colour and subject differentiation;
3. exact typography scale and usage;
4. complete form/validation/status semantics;
5. social format templates; and
6. video/motion template system.
