## Decision

The Founder explicitly approved **Brand Studio v0.5 direction**.

The approval covers the recurring cross-channel brand grammar proposed in `research/brand-studio/brand-studio-channels-motion-assets-v0.5.md`:

1. seven marketing/editorial pattern families;
2. Admin shell/table/filter/status/destructive pattern rules;
3. five social format families and canonical working canvases;
4. video opening/title/lower-third/caption/product-demo/end-card/transition rules;
5. REV motion timing/behaviour and reduced-motion equivalents;
6. simplified email/lifecycle visual treatment;
7. production identity/app/social/video asset-package requirements; and
8. canonical asset naming/export/lifecycle rules.

## Consequence

These recurring rules are promoted into the draft Brand System on PR #75. The major brand-grammar definition is therefore complete enough to move from exploration into production-readiness work.

This approval does **not** assert that concept-board artwork is a production vector or motion master and does **not** approve production styling implementation. Canonical editable/vector identity masters, exports, motion source and implementation work remain required.
