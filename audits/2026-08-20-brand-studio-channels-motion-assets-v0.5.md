## Proposal summary

v0.5 proposes reusable pattern families for marketing/editorial, Admin/operations, social, video and email/lifecycle, while deliberately keeping each channel's expression appropriate to its job.

It also proposes a bounded REV state-motion system with reduced-motion equivalents, and defines the production asset package required before concept-board artwork can be treated as implementation-ready identity.

## Key guardrails

- marketing may be more expressive but must not invent a separate campaign identity;
- Admin shares controls, semantic colours and icon language but remains denser and quieter;
- social formats use large type and stronger crops while preserving the same identity grammar;
- video motion is short, readable and purposeful rather than sci-fi spectacle;
- REV motion never delays useful content and is fully understandable without animation;
- concept image boards are visual references, not canonical vector/motion masters;
- exported assets use stable human-readable naming and record lifecycle/source/licensing information.
