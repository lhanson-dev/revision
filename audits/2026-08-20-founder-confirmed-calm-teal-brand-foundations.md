# Founder-confirmed Calm Teal brand foundations — 20 August 2026

**Status:** Founder-confirmed design decision captured for promotion through PR #75  
**Authority impact:** Updates the proposed Revision Brand System in `20-brand-and-experience/Visual Brand System.md`  
**Production impact:** None in this audit PR; implementation remains separate governed work

## Decision

The Founder confirmed that the following visual foundations are locked:

1. **Manrope** as the primary brand/product typeface.
2. **Calm Teal** as the core colour system, including first-class light and dark themes.
3. **REV Living E + soft halo** as the approved identity direction: three horizontal bars with a calm, subtle halo.
4. REV states: **Resting, Listening, Thinking, Responding, Completed**.
5. The responsive learner homepage layout shown across **mobile, tablet and desktop**, in both light and dark modes.

## Homepage layout captured

The approved visual hierarchy is:

- generous REV/Living E presence;
- concise personalised greeting / what-shall-we-do-today prompt;
- large REV input immediately beneath;
- a small quick-action layer;
- continue/recent-context content beneath; and
- progressively richer content through scroll rather than a dense dashboard above the fold.

The same hierarchy scales across mobile, tablet and desktop and is valid in both themes.

## Information-architecture conflict check

One desktop visual concept omits a separate `REV` navigation label. This does **not** change approved product Information Architecture.

The canonical learner destinations remain:

**Home / Plan / REV / Progress / Subjects**

The visual layout is approved subject to that governing navigation structure. Mobile already represents REV in the centre using the Living E treatment.

## Superseded exploration within PR #75

Where earlier Brand Studio experiments conflict with this decision, they are now historical exploration rather than the recommended direction:

- indigo/lime as the principal brand palette;
- Inter as the candidate baseline family;
- REV Cyan/Violet/Coral/Gold as the leading wider expressive palette; and
- E-stack/book-stack identity exploration that differs from the confirmed Living E + soft halo treatment.

Historical boards remain preserved; they must not be rewritten to look as though the Calm Teal decision existed earlier.
