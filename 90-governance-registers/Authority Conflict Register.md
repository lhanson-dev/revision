## ACR-006 — Delegated REC-10 privacy decision versus independent pre-production verification (22 October 2026)

**Status: OPEN — proposed phased reconciliation on draft PR #658; awaiting Founder-approved governance merge.** Founder requested that the AI product owner make a pragmatic privacy review decision for REC-10 while guaranteeing a final review before the product becomes publicly available. Existing `40-evidence-and-trust/Privacy and Student Data Principles.md` §12 requires independent validation of applicable legal/privacy requirements **before production reliance**, which includes processing real student data in a controlled pilot. Automatically postponing *all* privacy/legal diligence to public launch would conflict with that existing safeguard.

**Proposed resolution:** Allow isolated synthetic engineering evaluation without collecting live child data; require a documented accountable children's-data DPIA, lawful basis, transparency, rights and backup controls, and pre-pilot go/no-go before real-student processing; require an additional **independent qualified whole-product privacy review** as `PUBLIC-PRIV-01` before unrestricted public learner access. The AI's product-risk recommendation is not legal compliance certification. Proposed authority amendment is in privacy principles §12A and exam recovery. **Owner:** Founder (governance acceptance), accountable controller (pre-pilot) and qualified independent reviewer (public gate). No unmerged proposal overrides existing authority.
