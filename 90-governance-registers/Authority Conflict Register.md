Existing `40-evidence-and-trust/Privacy and Student Data Principles.md` §12 requires independent validation of applicable legal/privacy requirements **before production reliance**, which includes processing real student data in a controlled pilot. Proposed authority amendment is in privacy principles §12A and exam recovery. **Owner:** Founder (governance acceptance), accountable controller (pre-pilot) and qualified independent reviewer (public gate). No unmerged proposal overrides existing authority.
