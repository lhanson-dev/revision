# Marketing Governance

Future home of marketing strategy, website information architecture, routes, SEO/search intent, content, social, email/lifecycle, campaigns and conversion rules.

Visual identity and cross-channel brand expression for the marketing website, campaigns, social media, email and video are governed by `20-brand-and-experience/Visual Brand System.md`. Marketing governance owns what the channel is for, what is communicated, how conversion/publishing operates and which claims/messages are permitted; the Brand System owns how Revision is visually expressed within those channels.
