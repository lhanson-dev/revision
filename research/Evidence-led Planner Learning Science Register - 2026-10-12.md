## Register operating contract
Each active entry requires: stable ID, source and checked date, evidence quality, educational population/task boundaries, what it supports, what it **does not** establish, candidate rule, actual promoted authority/rule once approved, implementation owner, code/config/tests (once implemented), learner/product outcome validation, last review and change note. Absence of a verified source is tagged **hypothesis** rather than disguised as research. Study effect sizes must never be marketed as measured effects of Revision.

## Claims boundary
Potentially supportable with accurate implementation and precise wording: 'Revision draws on research into retrieval practice, spaced study and feedback to help you decide what to revise'; 'Your plan adjusts as exam dates, available time and demonstrated practice change.' Claims requiring product-specific controlled or carefully qualified outcomes research: 'Revision raises grades', 'science-proven optimal timetable', 'X% better exam results', 'knows exactly what you will remember'. Neither the cited papers nor deterministic simulation tests establish these.
