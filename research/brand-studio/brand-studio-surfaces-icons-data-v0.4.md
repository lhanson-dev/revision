# Brand Studio v0.4 — surfaces, iconography, subject accents and data visualisation

**Status:** Founder-confirmed direction — approved 20 August 2026  
**Authority:** Recurring rules are promoted into the draft `20-brand-and-experience/Visual Brand System.md`; this file remains design evidence/reference  
**Approved foundation:** Manrope, Calm Teal, Living E, light/dark themes, v0.3 typography/spacing/radius/elevation/controls/forms

## Purpose

Define the next layer of the system: enough recurring visual grammar to keep cards, icons, subjects and progress/evidence views consistent while preserving creative freedom.

## 1. Surface families

### Standard
- Job: ordinary content grouping.
- Radius: 20px.
- Light: white + Neutral 200 border; dark: standard dark surface + border.
- Depth: Flat by default.
- Padding: 20–24px.
- Avoid: turning every piece of content into a card.

### Quiet / supporting
- Job: secondary information, helper content, low-priority context.
- Radius: 20px.
- Light: Soft Surface Tint / Neutral 100; dark: subtle surface step.
- Depth: Flat.
- Avoid: important primary actions that need stronger hierarchy.

### Interactive
- Job: route, choice, subject/topic selection or actionable summary.
- Radius: 20px.
- Depth: Flat at rest, Raised on hover/focus/selection where appropriate.
- Must show affordance through layout/icon/arrow/label, not hover alone.
- Whole-card click behaviour should not conflict with nested controls.

### Feature / editorial
- Job: exceptional brand moment, marketing storytelling, major learner feature.
- Radius: 32px.
- May use Deep Teal, Soft Aqua, stronger typography, illustration or asymmetric composition.
- Avoid: repetitive grids of equally loud feature cards.

### Guidance / recommendation
- Job: next step, explanation, coach-style recommendation.
- Radius: 20px or 32px according to prominence.
- Usually Soft Aqua / quiet teal treatment with clear action.
- If recommendation is evidence-based, explain why; do not use visual confidence to overstate evidence.

### REV
- Job: REV conversation, recommendation and AI presence only.
- Radius: 32px for hero/major treatments, 20px compact context.
- Living E + soft halo is the distinctive device.
- Ordinary product cards must not borrow the halo simply to look important.

### Status / feedback
- Job: success, warning, error, information.
- Radius: 14–20px.
- Use approved semantic surface + strong foreground + icon + text.
- Never use status colour as decoration or as the sole carrier of meaning.

### Exam / performance
- Job: timed work, marks, paper readiness and performance evidence.
- Radius: 20px.
- Structured and calm; Deep Teal/Graphite anchors with restrained Stone Blue/Warm Sand support.
- Must not manufacture urgency or imply certainty unsupported by evidence.

### Subject accent
- Job: fast recognition of subject context.
- Radius: 20px standard surface.
- Main surface stays neutral; accent appears in icon tile, small edge marker, chip or illustration detail.
- Never turn each subject into a separate full-page theme.

### Pricing / upgrade
- Job: explain package value and entitlement boundary.
- Radius: 20px ordinary / 32px featured plan.
- Paid emphasis may use stronger teal/deep-teal hierarchy.
- Locked state must explain benefit and entitlement rather than rely on a padlock or faded UI.

## 2. Iconography

Use a consistent rounded line language:

- default UI icon: 24px;
- compact: 20px;
- inline: 16px where legible;
- large illustrative/icon tile: 28–32px;
- default stroke: 1.75–2px, rounded caps and joins;
- icons inherit `currentColor` and should not hard-code arbitrary colours;
- selected/status icons may use fill selectively but retain a clear silhouette;
- REV Living E is a custom identity mark, not a generic icon.

Avoid mixed icon libraries, emoji as product controls, cartoon clip-art, inconsistent 3D icons and decorative symbols with unclear meaning.

## 3. Graphic language

Approved motifs:

- soft halo / diffused circle around REV only;
- three-line / stacked-line echoes derived from the Living E;
- restrained concentric rings or ripple traces for focus/listening states;
- rounded tabs, underlines and marker bars that subtly reference studying/revision;
- small dot/trace accents used sparingly to suggest activity or connection;
- soft geometric crops and overlapping rounded fields for marketing/editorial composition.

Use one or two motifs per composition, not all at once. Avoid heavy sci-fi particles, neon bloom, school clip-art and decorative complexity that competes with content.

## 4. Subject accents

Do **not** assign semantic success/warning/error colours to subjects.

Use the approved supporting palette as a recognition layer rather than a theme system:

- Soft Aqua Accent;
- Sage;
- Stone Blue;
- Warm Sand;
- Mist;
- Primary Teal may be used as a shared brand accent rather than a unique subject colour.

Subject identity must combine **name + icon + optional accent**. Colour alone is never sufficient, and two subjects may reuse an accent when the icon/name remain clear.

No fixed subject-to-colour mapping is approved. Introduce exact mapping only if user testing shows it materially improves recognition.

## 5. Data visualisation

### Core rule
Charts explain evidence; they do not decorate dashboards.

Use:
- progress bar for bounded part-to-whole completion;
- line chart for change over time;
- bar chart for comparison;
- stacked bar only when composition genuinely matters;
- donut only for a small number of categories where precise comparison is not important;
- sparkline only as supplemental context, never the only explanation.

Every material progress/readiness view must provide plain-English meaning and a next action where appropriate.

### Evidence semantics
Never collapse these into one decorative percentage:
- coverage;
- understanding/mastery evidence;
- exam readiness;
- plan progress.

A trend may legitimately move down when new evidence changes the picture. Avoid visual metaphors that imply progress can only increase.

### Approved derived data-series palette
These are chart colours, not new brand identity colours:

1. Data Teal `#168C7C`
2. Data Blue `#5F7FA3`
3. Data Sage `#5F8F73`
4. Data Sand `#A5824A`
5. Data Slate `#607074`

Each provides at least approximately 3:1 contrast against both the light canvas and dark background for graphical objects. When three or more series appear, also use direct labels, markers, line styles or patterns so meaning is not colour-dependent.

Primary Teal `#2BB6A3` remains excellent as a fill/accent but is too low-contrast against white for a thin standalone line carrying essential information.

### Progress and status
- Neutral progress uses Data Teal/Deep Teal, not Success green by default.
- Success/Warning/Error are used only when the underlying status genuinely has that semantic meaning.
- Estimated grades/readiness must be explicitly labelled as estimates where governed evidence requires it.
- "On track" must state on track for what.

## 6. Cross-channel expression

- Learner: Standard, Quiet, Interactive and REV families dominate; Feature surfaces remain scarce.
- Marketing: Feature/editorial surfaces and graphic motifs can be more expressive.
- Admin: Standard/Quiet surfaces, compact radius where needed, limited shadow, dense tables outside card overload.
- Social/video: use Feature composition and graphic motifs, but retain Manrope, Calm Teal and Living E recognition.

## Founder decision

On 20 August 2026 the Founder explicitly approved the Brand Studio v0.4 direction, including:

1. the ten named surface families and their jobs;
2. the rounded line icon language and size/stroke rules;
3. restrained Living-E-derived graphic motifs;
4. subject accents as small recognition cues using the approved supporting palette, without fixed mapping;
5. evidence-first chart selection rules;
6. the five-colour derived data-series palette; and
7. strict separation of coverage, understanding/mastery evidence, exam readiness and plan progress in visualisation.

This confirms the recurring grammar, not every future card layout, subject design or marketing composition.
