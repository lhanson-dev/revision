# Brand Studio v0.5 — channels, REV motion and production assets

**Status:** Founder-approved direction; promoted into `20-brand-and-experience/Visual Brand System.md` v0.9  
**Proposal date:** 20 August 2026  
**Approved foundation:** Manrope, Calm Teal, Living E, light/dark themes, v0.3 foundations/controls/forms and v0.4 surfaces/icons/subject/data rules

## Purpose

Complete the remaining cross-channel grammar so Revision can move from a learner-app visual system to a usable company-wide brand system without forcing every channel to look identical.

This tranche covers:

1. marketing/editorial patterns;
2. Admin/operations patterns;
3. social formats;
4. video and motion graphics;
5. email/lifecycle treatment;
6. REV motion behaviour and reduced-motion equivalents; and
7. production asset/export governance.

It does **not** claim that concept-board artwork is a production master logo or motion asset. Canonical vector/editable masters are still required before implementation is complete.

## Founder decision

The Founder explicitly approved the v0.5 direction on 20 August 2026. The recurring rules below are now represented normatively in Brand System v0.9 on PR #75. This research file remains the detailed proposal/design reference rather than becoming a second source of authority.

## 1. Marketing / editorial pattern families

Marketing may be more expressive than the learner product, but should still look unmistakably Revision.

### A. Hero / proposition

Use for the primary page proposition.

- generous 64–96px vertical breathing room where viewport supports it;
- Display XL / Display L Manrope hierarchy;
- one dominant primary CTA plus at most one secondary action;
- Feature surface or open-canvas editorial composition;
- Living E/REV may appear when the proposition is genuinely REV-led, not as mandatory decoration;
- product UI/screenshots should be framed as evidence of the product rather than as an ornamental collage.

Avoid multiple equal CTAs, dense feature grids above the fold and overuse of the halo.

### B. Feature story

Use for explaining a meaningful learner/parent benefit.

- asymmetric two-column or stacked composition;
- one strong visual or product example;
- H2/H3 hierarchy plus short supporting copy;
- Standard/Feature/Guidance surface families according to emphasis;
- alternate image/text position to create editorial rhythm rather than repeating identical cards.

### C. Product proof / demonstration

Use for screenshots, walkthroughs, results or evidence.

- use real or clearly labelled representative UI;
- annotate only the important point;
- do not invent performance claims merely to make a mock-up persuasive;
- keep evidence semantics aligned with Claims and Progress Governance.

### D. Pricing / packaging

- Pricing/Upgrade family governs cards;
- ordinary plan cards use Standard radius; featured plan may use Feature radius;
- daily/monthly/annual hierarchy may vary according to approved pricing authority, but visual prominence must not make price information misleading;
- locked capability explanations focus on additional learner value rather than shame or anxiety.

### E. Proof / testimonial / quote

- Quiet or Feature surface depending on prominence;
- quote should remain readable and attributable;
- do not use decorative quotation marks as the main brand motif;
- evidence/claims remain subject to marketing/evidence governance.

### F. Editorial / guide

- reading width 680–760px;
- strong typography and spacing rather than card-wrapping every paragraph;
- optional inline diagrams, note surfaces or REV guidance where context genuinely benefits.

### G. Closing CTA

- one dominant action;
- Deep Teal or Soft Aqua Feature surface is preferred over introducing a new campaign colour;
- enough whitespace to feel intentional rather than like another feature block.

## 2. Admin / operations pattern families

Admin should feel like Revision but optimise for operational efficiency rather than learner theatre.

### A. Admin shell

- compact but readable Manrope hierarchy;
- Neutral/Soft Surface foundation with Primary Teal used for action/selection, not decoration;
- no Living E halo unless an actual REV/AI function is present;
- persistent navigation may be denser than learner navigation while preserving shared icon/control language.

### B. Tables

- tables are first-class; do not force every row into a card;
- 12px Compact radius where grouping requires it;
- sticky headings/columns allowed when useful;
- row actions use clear icon + label/accessibility semantics;
- sorting/filtering state must remain obvious without colour alone.

### C. Filters and search

- shared Control radius and form anatomy;
- compact 36px controls may be used where pointer/keyboard density is appropriate, but touch-exposed surfaces retain larger targets;
- active filters use chips/pills with explicit labels and removal controls.

### D. Status and assurance

- semantic colours retain exactly the same meaning as elsewhere;
- status cards use Status family, not colourful dashboard tiles;
- Unknown/Not checked are visually distinct from Success;
- operational severity and educational evidence semantics must not be conflated.

### E. Critical/destructive actions

- Deep Teal/Graphite remains default action language;
- destructive operations use Error semantics and explicit confirmation where consequence warrants it;
- irreversible actions must not rely on position or colour alone.

## 3. Social format families

Social uses stronger crops and bigger typography while retaining the same identity.

### Standard production canvases

- square feed: **1080×1080**;
- portrait feed: **1080×1350**;
- story/reel: **1080×1920**;
- landscape/video thumbnail: **1920×1080**.

Platform-specific exports may vary, but these are the canonical working templates unless a channel requires another size.

### A. Brand statement / message

- large Manrope statement;
- one Living-E-derived motif or soft geometric crop;
- small wordmark/identity anchor;
- usually 8–20 words rather than paragraphs.

### B. Study tip / educational value

- short title + one concise useful point;
- calm supporting icon/diagram;
- should remain useful even without a sales CTA;
- avoid mimicking school worksheets.

### C. Product showcase

- real product crop/device frame;
- one clear annotation or benefit statement;
- Calm Teal canvas and consistent screenshot framing.

### D. Proof / statistic

- one primary number/claim + plain-language context;
- source/evidence reference where required;
- never overstate a statistic for visual impact.

### E. Launch / campaign

- Feature-level expression permitted;
- may use larger graphic crops, Deep Teal fields and more motion;
- must still use Manrope, Calm Teal and approved motifs rather than inventing a separate campaign identity.

### Social rules

- keep key text inside safe areas, especially story/reel interfaces;
- do not use the Living E as a random bullet/icon;
- do not make REV halo/glow the background of every post;
- captions/alt text should carry essential information when the visual itself is not sufficient.

## 4. Video and motion-graphics system

### A. Opening sting

Recommended default: **0.8–1.5 seconds**.

- Living E or Revision wordmark enters cleanly;
- soft halo/pulse rather than neon explosion;
- no compulsory sound dependency;
- avoid delaying the useful content for brand theatre.

### B. Title card

- Manrope H1/Display hierarchy;
- Calm Teal/Canvas or Deep Teal foundation;
- one supporting motif maximum;
- title, topic and optional qualification/series label.

### C. Lower third

- 14px/16px equivalent readable text at target resolution;
- Soft Surface or Deep Teal compact bar with 12–14px corner family;
- never cover key learning content;
- duration long enough to read comfortably.

### D. Captions/subtitles

- high-contrast text with solid/semi-opaque background where video content reduces contrast;
- avoid all-caps paragraphs;
- keep line length and placement readable on mobile crops;
- captions are functional, not decorative.

### E. Product demo frame

- show product at legible scale;
- pointer/highlight treatment uses Primary/Data Teal and simple ring/marker rather than glow everywhere;
- zoom/crop only when it improves comprehension.

### F. End card

Recommended default: **2–3 seconds** where distribution format allows.

- Revision wordmark / Living E relationship;
- one CTA or destination;
- Deep Teal or Canvas foundation;
- avoid multiple social handles/URLs competing equally.

### G. Transitions

- prefer cuts, soft fades, short slides and Living-E-line wipes;
- typical branded transition should remain around **160–320ms** in product-style motion and may extend modestly in editorial video;
- avoid spins, bounce-heavy motion, glitch, scanlines and sci-fi particle transitions.

## 5. REV motion system

REV motion must communicate state rather than act as ambient spectacle.

### Resting

- bars stable and evenly spaced;
- halo breathes gently on a **6–8 second** loop;
- amplitude should be subtle enough not to demand attention;
- optional sparse dot/trace drift must not look like a notification.

### Listening

- bars remain recognisable;
- a soft outward ripple or slight equalised movement confirms attention;
- **1.2–1.8 second** repeating rhythm while listening;
- do not use aggressive microphone-style waveform animation unless context genuinely needs it.

### Thinking

- bars may stagger/shift in a controlled sequence;
- halo/ring movement becomes slightly more active;
- **1.4–2.2 second** loop;
- must not imply progress percentage or estimated completion time.

### Responding

- the three bars may carry a left-to-right or centre-out pulse as response begins;
- transition into responding around **600–900ms**;
- response text must not be delayed to complete the animation;
- typing animation, if used, should be fast and skippable/compatible with reduced motion.

### Completed

- bars settle back to equal alignment;
- halo briefly expands/softens and returns to Resting;
- **700–1000ms** settle;
- do not turn completion into confetti or gamified celebration by default.

### Reduced motion

When `prefers-reduced-motion` or equivalent preference is active:

- no looping halo breathe, ripple or bar oscillation;
- use static visual variants for Resting/Listening/Thinking/Responding/Completed;
- state change may use instant swap or short opacity change no longer than approximately 100–150ms;
- text/icon labels or context must make state understandable without animation.

### Performance rule

REV motion should be lightweight enough that it never blocks interaction, causes layout shifts or materially delays first useful paint. Motion implementation technology remains an engineering decision; the Brand System governs appearance/behaviour, not a specific animation library.

## 6. Email / lifecycle visual treatment

Email is a constrained channel and should use a simplified expression of the same system.

- Revision wordmark at top; Living E only where REV is genuinely the sender/context;
- white/Canvas background with Deep Teal text;
- Primary Teal + Graphite CTA treatment where client support/contrast remains safe;
- 14px control-like CTA radius rather than oversized app-card radii;
- Status family may be simplified to border + icon + text;
- no reliance on gradients, animation or advanced CSS for essential meaning;
- dark-mode email support should be tested rather than assumed from app tokens.

## 7. Production identity and asset package

The supplied concept boards are **approved visual references**, not canonical production masters.

Before production implementation is considered complete, the canonical asset package should contain:

### Revision identity

- primary Revision wordmark — editable vector master;
- light-background SVG;
- dark-background SVG;
- monochrome dark SVG;
- monochrome light/reversed SVG;
- compact/approved lock-up if one exists;
- minimum-size and clear-space guidance.

### REV / Living E

- static Living E master SVG;
- light-theme variant;
- dark-theme variant;
- small/nav variant preserving three-bar legibility;
- static files for Resting / Listening / Thinking / Responding / Completed where state differs visually;
- motion source for the approved state system once animation implementation is selected;
- reduced-motion/static state equivalents.

### App / browser

- app-icon master at **1024×1024** or platform-equivalent vector/master source;
- generated platform icon exports rather than manually redrawing at each size;
- favicon SVG where supported;
- 32×32 and 16×16 raster fallback where required;
- maskable/PWA-safe treatment if the web app requires it.

### Social / media templates

- 1080×1080;
- 1080×1350;
- 1080×1920;
- 1920×1080;
- editable source plus export preset;
- safe-zone guides where platform UI overlaps content.

### Video

- title card master;
- lower-third master;
- caption style reference;
- opening/end-card master;
- transparent motion exports only when a destination workflow needs them.

## 8. Canonical naming and lifecycle rules

Use human-readable, stable names. Avoid spaces encoded as `%20` in exported filenames.

Recommended pattern:

`revision-{asset}-{variant}-{theme}-{size}.{ext}`

Examples:

- `revision-wordmark-primary-light.svg`
- `revision-wordmark-mono-dark.svg`
- `revision-rev-living-e-resting-light.svg`
- `revision-rev-living-e-nav-dark.svg`
- `revision-app-icon-1024.png`
- `revision-social-product-1080x1350.png`
- `revision-video-lower-third-light.svg`

Do not add version numbers to ordinary production filenames unless a distribution channel requires immutable versioning. Version/history belongs in source control/asset management, not in a trail of `final-v7-really-final` files.

Each canonical asset entry should record:

- lifecycle status: Recommended / Alternative / Experimental / Deprecated / Do not use;
- editable source location;
- export location or generation method;
- intended channels;
- theme/variant rules;
- licensing/source information for third-party materials; and
- replacement/deprecation relationship where applicable.
