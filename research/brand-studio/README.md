# Brand Studio — visual confirmation workspace

**Status:** Experimental research / Founder confirmation aid  
**Authority:** None. This folder does not become brand authority by being rendered.  
**Governed source:** `20-brand-and-experience/Visual Brand System.md` once approved and merged.

## Purpose

Provide a single visual place to inspect proposed Revision brand grammar before it is promoted into approved authority or production implementation.

The Brand Studio should help answer two questions:

1. **Does this clearly feel like Revision?**
2. **Is the system controlled enough for consistency while leaving enough freedom for good design?**

## Explicitly not approved by these boards

- final logo/wordmark geometry or lock-ups;
- final E-stack geometry;
- app icon/favicon/social-avatar exports;
- final minimum-size / clear-space rules;
- a complete expressive palette;
- exact subject-to-colour mapping;
- final semantic token palette;
- final display typeface or licensing model;
- final type scale and all weights;
- production social templates;
- video animation timings/transitions; and
- production CSS/component or Brand Studio implementation.

## Confirmation method

The Founder should confirm or adjust the **grammar and range**, not approve every future composition.

For each recurring pattern, use:

- **Recommended** — default for the stated job;
- **Alternative** — approved where context justifies it;
- **Experimental** — visible for evaluation but not reusable authority;
- **Deprecated** — retained during migration but not for new work; or
- **Do not use** — rejected/non-compliant treatment.

Once a recurring treatment is approved, update the normative Brand System through the governed PR. The rendered Brand Studio should then reflect that approved authority and link to canonical implementation/assets.
