## Evidence consequence

The compulsory-course search now shows a repeatable pattern:

- broad psychological foundations are often available under clean CC BY terms;
- AQA-specific named formulations are much less consistently available in a single commercially reusable source;
- narrow statistical and research-method gaps can often be covered by page-level CC BY/CC BY-SA material, but rights must be checked at the actual item used;
- several exact AQA teaching constructs still require a small bridge or new targeted source rather than whole-topic generation.

No paid provider or paid source was used for this pass.
