# Psychology Source Register — Source-First Prototype

**Course proof:** AQA A-level Psychology 7182
**Started:** 2 October 2026
**Status:** Active prototype evidence

This register records source candidates discovered during the source-first prototype. It is intentionally separate from the production Content Factory Source Licence Register and does not imply production approval.

## Classification used by this prototype

- `ACCEPTED_TEXT_CANDIDATE` — source-level licence explicitly permits commercial reuse/adaptation; textual material may be used for the prototype subject corpus subject to exclusions/attribution noted by the source.
- `PARTIAL/MIXED` — some material is commercially reusable but item-level licence separation is required.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY` — useful for scope/facts but not used as reusable teaching corpus.
- `REJECTED_NC` — non-commercial licence makes it unsuitable for Revision's commercial reusable corpus.
- `REJECTED_AI` — source terms explicitly prohibit or require separate permission for AI ingestion/use.
- `PENDING` — rights or source details require further verification.

## Early conclusion — 2 October 2026

The initial search already disproves the assumption that the only broad free psychology textbooks are non-commercial. Multiple substantial psychology texts are available under CC BY 4.0 and appear to cover significant portions of the AQA 7182 subject knowledge.

The most promising starting corpus is currently:

1. `Psychology — H5P Edition` (BCcampus) as broad core coverage;
2. `Introduction to Psychology & Neuroscience` (Dalhousie) as broad corroboration / neuroscience depth;
3. CC BY abnormal-psychology resources for clinical, schizophrenia, eating/addiction-related depth;
4. additional topic-specific CC BY material only where the AQA mapping exposes gaps.

The next prototype step is not more infrastructure. It is to map this accepted corpus against the AQA 7182 requirements and quantify `covered / partial / gap / uncertain`.
