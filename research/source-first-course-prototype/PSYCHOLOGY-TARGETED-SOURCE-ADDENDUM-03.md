## Accepted sources

### Minority Influence: An Agenda for Study of Social Change — Prislin (2022)

- Source: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.911654
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9262047/
- Journal: *Frontiers in Psychology*.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).
- Commercial reuse: yes.
- Adaptation: yes with attribution.
- Prototype status: `ACCEPTED_TEXT_CANDIDATE`.
- Named AQA relevance: the paper describes minority behavioural style through temporal consistency, internal consensus, autonomy/investment in the position, and sufficient open-mindedness to avoid rigidity. These provide reusable subject knowledge for the AQA consistency/commitment/flexibility requirement without using AQA teaching prose.

### Fisher, Neyman-Pearson or NHST? A tutorial for teaching data testing — Perezgonzalez (2015)

- Source: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00223
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4347431/
- Journal: *Frontiers in Psychology*.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).
- Commercial reuse: yes.
- Adaptation: yes with attribution.
- Prototype status: `ACCEPTED_TEXT_CANDIDATE`.
- Named AQA relevance: explicitly distinguishes directional and non-directional hypotheses and connects directionality to one-sided/two-sided testing.

### What Pilot Studies Can (and Cannot) Do for Validity in Psychological Research — Seetahul et al. (2026 preprint)

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/t968e_v2
- Licence metadata: CC BY 4.0.
- Commercial reuse: yes under the recorded licence.
- Adaptation: yes with attribution.
- Prototype status: `ACCEPTED_TEXT_CANDIDATE` for the preprint version carrying the CC BY 4.0 licence.
- Named AQA relevance: defines piloting as methodological due diligence before a main study and identifies design, measurement, manipulation, sampling and analysis decisions that a pilot can test or refine.
- Caution: retain exact version/DOI and licence evidence because this is a preprint and may later have a differently licensed version of record.

### Must We Always Go Idiographic? — Kaiser et al. (2026)

- Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10608-025-10672-4
- Journal: *Cognitive Therapy and Research*.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0.

### Difficult Research Conversations: Sharing Socially Sensitive Research in the Public Domain — Babacan & Babacan

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.25120/etropic.12.1.2013.3393

## Reconciliation consequence

The six compulsory requirements previously labelled `gap` are no longer six broad content gaps:

- five now have rights-clear reusable subject evidence;
- one (`PSY-07-19`) is a small AQA-specific application/alignment bridge over subject knowledge already covered elsewhere;
- no compulsory requirement currently requires buying a source or running paid generation merely to establish the underlying subject truth.

This does **not** mean the compulsory Foundation is production-ready. Thirty-eight requirements still need some degree of bridge/supplementation, and those bridge sizes are assessed separately.
