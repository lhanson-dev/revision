**Purpose:** Record targeted sources discovered only after the coarse coverage map exposed specific weak areas. This addendum supplements `PSYCHOLOGY-SOURCE-REGISTER.md`; it does not imply production approval.

## Rights rule

Only material with an explicit commercial-compatible licence is counted as reusable subject-corpus evidence. Mixed pages are fail-closed at item level: only the text carrying an explicit compatible attribution may be reused.

## Accepted targeted candidates

### Statistics for Research Students — University of Southern Queensland

- Source: https://usq.pressbooks.pub/statisticsforresearchstudents/
- Provider: University of Southern Queensland / UniSQ Open Textbooks.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, except where otherwise noted.
- Commercial reuse: yes.
- Adaptation: yes.
- Conditions: attribution; corporate logos/branding are excluded and separately noted material must be checked.
- Prototype status: `ACCEPTED_TEXT_CANDIDATE` for eligible text.
- Named AQA relevance: descriptive statistics, variable types, normality, statistical power, Type I/II errors, Mann–Whitney U, Wilcoxon signed-rank and broader test-selection/statistical reasoning.
- Current limitation: this is not, by itself, evidence for the complete AQA Research Methods list; sign-test calculation, exact AQA test-choice rules and psychology-specific design terminology still need separate evidence.

## Mixed / item-level candidate

### Criminology 1st Edition — Minnesota State Pressbooks

- Source: https://minnstate.pressbooks.pub/criminology/
- Book-level licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; therefore the book as a whole is **not** acceptable for Revision's commercial corpus.
- Prototype status: `PARTIAL/MIXED` only.
- Important exception: individual sections on the site carry their own CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0 attributions. Only those explicitly re-licensed sections may be considered, and each must retain its own item-level provenance.
- Relevant item-level evidence found:
  - the `Crime and Intelligence` section within the psychological-theories chapter is explicitly attributed CC BY 4.0 and discusses Eysenck's criminal-personality account;
  - the `Interactionist Theories` / social-learning material within the social-process chapter is explicitly attributed CC BY 4.0 and includes Sutherland's differential association theory.
- Named AQA relevance: targeted support for parts of `PSY-16-03`.
- Constraint: do not ingest surrounding chapter/book text merely because the required section appears on the same page.

## Evidence consequence

The targeted search changes the early conclusion in two useful ways:

1. **Research Methods is less gap-heavy than the coarse map implied.** A commercially reusable CC BY 4.0 statistics textbook now covers a meaningful portion of the quantitative/statistical requirement, reducing reliance on the CC BY-SA `Learning Statistics with R` candidate.
2. **Forensic Psychology is not a single-source solution, but it is no longer evidence-free.** Commercially reusable criminal-justice material plus CC BY 4.0 profiling research can support parts of the option. The remaining problem is the AQA-specific synthesis of profiling frameworks and the psychology of offending/treatment, not a total absence of permitted sources.

No paid provider or paid source was used for this pass.
