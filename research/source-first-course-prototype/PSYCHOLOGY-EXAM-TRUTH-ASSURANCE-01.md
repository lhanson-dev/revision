# Psychology Exam Truth Assurance 01

**Date:** 4 October 2026  
**Course:** AQA A-level Psychology 7182, revised specification, first A-level exams Summer 2027  
**Prototype:** Source-First Course Prototype  
**Purpose:** Record the bounded assessment-alignment challenge used to complete experimental Exam Truth.

## Inputs checked

Current official AQA web authority was checked for:

- qualification/version identity;
- specification-at-a-glance paper structure and topic/option scope;
- scheme-of-assessment AO definitions and weighting ranges;
- research-method and mathematical assessment requirements;
- current Psychology command words; and
- AQA's change notice for first teaching September 2025.

AQA assessment guidance for specification 7182 was used only for question-family and tariff calibration where the current specification does not state a fixed future-paper rule.

All AQA material is retained as `REFERENCE_ONLY`. The Exam Truth contains structured factual alignment only; it does not promote protected AQA question or mark-scheme prose into a reusable corpus.

## Deterministic reconciliation target

The committed validator requires the Exam Truth to prove:

- exactly three papers: `7182/1`, `7182/2`, `7182/3`;
- 120 minutes and 96 attempted raw marks for each paper;
- 288 raw marks across the qualification and x1 scaling;
- Paper 1 sections of 24 marks across topics 1–4;
- Paper 2 sections of 24, 24 and 48 marks across topics 5–7;
- Paper 3 Section A plus three 24-mark option-group sections, each requiring one of three topics;
- exact primary coverage of topic numbers 1–17 without omission or duplication;
- current AO1, AO2 and AO3 overall and per-paper contribution ranges;
- 25–30% overall research-method assessment;
- at least 10% overall mathematical-skills assessment;
- research-method and mathematical skills remain assessable across all three papers;
- six bounded Revision question-family classes;
- the 28 current AQA Psychology command words represented once in the Revision demand model; and
- all awarding-body sources fail closed as `REFERENCE_ONLY`, with protected source text excluded from downstream generation.

## Assessment-pattern challenge

A material risk was overfitting historical papers into invented future invariants.

AQA's 2024 change notice states that the 2025 subject-content revision did not change assessment, question types or paper structure. That supports continued use of existing AQA 7182 assessment guidance as **calibration**. It does not justify asserting that a future paper must repeat a historical command sequence, topic order, dataset, exact number of maximum-tariff essays or other observed pattern that the current specification does not guarantee.

The Exam Truth therefore separates:

- **current invariants** — paper/component structure, section marks, topic/option ownership, duration, weighting, AO ranges, research-method and maths constraints; from
- **calibration evidence** — familiar question families, variable extended-writing tariffs and maximum-tariff guidance.

The model explicitly prohibits cloning a historical paper as the rule for Revision mock production.

## Extended-writing calibration

Current AQA Psychology assessment guidance identifies multiple-choice, short-answer, stem/scenario application and extended-writing families. It also identifies 16 marks as the A-level maximum extended-writing tariff, with no fixed rule for the number of maximum-tariff essays on a paper, and indicates that shorter extended-writing tariffs can be used.

The Exam Truth records this as calibration rather than a guaranteed future-paper count.

## Component-placement finding

The official specification assigns genuinely different content to the three A-level papers. Psychology therefore meets the `genuine_component_specific_content` case in the existing Course Content and Assessment Component Placement authority.

This finding changes no product authority. It applies the existing rule to this course. Course Truth remains single-source; paper scope becomes assessment metadata and a downstream Blueprint input.

## Current limitations

- Updated Sample Assessment Materials for the revised specification are identified by AQA as available through Centre Services. They were not used as a reusable corpus and are not required to establish the published structural invariants recorded here.
- Exact future-paper mixes inside the published section/AO constraints remain intentionally non-deterministic. Later mock production must calibrate representativeness without converting past-paper frequency into false certainty.
- Fresh independent assessment/authenticity assurance has not yet run; under the active student-pilot sequence that remains a later production-level gate before restricted-pilot publication.

## Outcome

No material assessment-alignment or source-rights blocker is known for the intended Exam Truth scope.

`examTruthComplete=true` is therefore appropriate once the deterministic repository assurance passes on the governed branch.

This does not imply learner-asset readiness or publication approval.
