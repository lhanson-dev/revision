## Completeness-pass evidence frozen with the seal

### Pass 1 — subject universe and content build

The research used Ofqual, UK government/legislation, ONS, Bank of England and recognised professional bodies across accounting/finance, marketing, HR/people, management and procurement to establish the initial subject universe, teaching content, quantitative methods, models, misconceptions and cross-functional relationships.

### Pass 2 — deliberate gap hunt

Different source families including OECD, ILO, FRC, CMA, HBS and BCG were used to challenge the first pass. The research records material strengthening in evidence literacy, governance/management separation, employee voice, resilience/supply dependency, digital transformation as capability/process change, and the treatment of named frameworks as conditional analytical tools rather than factual laws.

### Pass 3 — saturation challenge

Further ONS management evidence, OECD digital/AI work, current UK consumer/sustainability material and deeper finance/procurement sources increasingly produced specialist depth, examples or advanced material rather than new general Level 3 Business domains. Residual uncertainties were retained rather than forced closed.
