## Purpose

Challenge the independently researched 80-node Business foundation against current major UK A-level / Level 3 Business qualifications without allowing awarding-body specifications to redefine the independent subject taxonomy retrospectively.

Awarding-body sources are treated as qualification-alignment / REFERENCE_ONLY evidence. Their protected prose is not reused as the foundation's teaching corpus.

## Qualifications checked

- A-level Business 7132 remains the outgoing course for cohorts taking final exams in 2027.
- A-level Business 7138 is accredited for first teaching from September 2026 and first A-level exams in summer 2028.

## Overall finding

The independent foundation performed well: the awarding-body comparison did **not** reveal a missing major Business domain or require restructuring the eight-domain taxonomy.

However, the comparison did expose several cases where the sealed foundation is either missing a reusable method/facet entirely or contains the correct parent concept but at insufficient breadth/depth for one or more current A-level qualifications.

The correct response is therefore **targeted subject-foundation expansion**, not rebuilding Business from any board specification.

## Delta classifications

### D-001 — Income elasticity of demand (YED)

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-MKT-008` Pricing, elasticity and revenue effects  
**Evidence:** AQA 7132, Cambridge OCR H436, Pearson Edexcel and Eduqas/WJEC all require income elasticity alongside price elasticity.

The sealed foundation teaches price elasticity but does not separately teach income elasticity in sufficient detail. The parent concept is already correct; the gap is an additional elasticity facet.

Future candidate content should teach:

- meaning of YED;
- formula and sign;
- normal versus inferior products;
- relative responsiveness to income change;
- calculation where course depth requires it;
- interpretation and demand / revenue implications;
- forecasting and capacity / inventory / workforce implications;
- limitations and instability of historic elasticity estimates.

### D-002 — Network analysis / Critical Path Analysis

**Classification:** `GENUINE_SUBJECT_FOUNDATION_GAP`  
**Suggested placement:** Evidence/Decision-making or Operations/Project planning  
**Evidence:** AQA 7132 explicitly requires network diagrams, critical path and total float; Pearson Edexcel and Eduqas/WJEC also require Critical Path Analysis.

The sealed foundation contains decision trees, forecasting and whole-business decision quality, but not the distinct project-planning method of network analysis.

Future candidate content should teach:

- activities and dependencies;
- sequencing and parallel work;
- earliest start / latest finish concepts where appropriate;
- activity duration;
- critical path;
- total float;
- interpretation of delays;
- resource / quality / time trade-offs;
- limitations of estimated durations and changing projects;
- connections to capacity, delegation, operations, cash flow and implementation.

### D-003 — Sales forecasting methods

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-EVI-003` Forecasting, extrapolation, scenarios and sensitivity  
**Evidence:** Cambridge OCR H436, Pearson Edexcel, Eduqas and WJEC explicitly require particular quantitative / qualitative sales-forecasting techniques.

The sealed foundation has the correct general forecasting concept but insufficient method detail.

Future candidate facets should include, at appropriate Level 3 depth:

- historical sales series and trend;
- scatter plots / line of best fit;
- extrapolation;
- time-series interpretation;
- moving averages where required;
- intuitive / qualitative forecasts;
- brainstorming and Delphi-type methods as optional framework-level content where justified;
- seasonality and structural change;
- forecast error and sensitivity;
- operational / financial decisions driven by forecasts.

### D-004 — Customer journey, CRM and customer-experience measurement

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed nodes:** `BUS-MKT-001`, `BUS-MKT-003`, `BUS-MKT-009`  
**Evidence:** Cambridge OCR H436 has an explicit customer-journey section; AQA 7138 explicitly includes customer relationship management and customer experience.

The sealed foundation already covers customer orientation, relationships and marketing channels. It needs a more explicit reusable facet for the end-to-end customer relationship.

Future candidate content should cover:

- journey stages as a useful model rather than universal truth;
- acquisition, purchase, service, retention and advocacy;
- customer experience across touchpoints;
- CRM purpose and data use;
- complaints / reviews / customer feedback;
- appropriate experience / loyalty measures;
- privacy, measurement and causality limitations;
- links between marketing promise and operations delivery.

### D-005 — Workforce performance metrics

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed nodes:** `BUS-PEO-001` Workforce planning and job design; `BUS-PEO-011` Culture, engagement, employee voice and relations  
**Evidence:** Pearson Edexcel and Eduqas/WJEC require labour productivity, labour turnover / retention and absenteeism; Cambridge OCR H436 explicitly requires measuring HR performance.

The sealed foundation has labour productivity and people concepts but lacks a complete workforce-measurement facet.

Future candidate content should include:

- labour turnover / retention;
- absenteeism;
- labour productivity linkage;
- calculation conventions where appropriate;
- interpretation by context and sector;
- why a high/low number is not automatically good/bad;
- causal ambiguity;
- interaction with recruitment, wellbeing, management, culture and labour cost.

### D-006 — Net Present Value / discounted investment appraisal depth

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-FIN-009` Investment appraisal  
**Evidence:** Pearson Edexcel, Eduqas/WJEC and CCEA require NPV at A-level depth; the sealed baseline deliberately retained NPV conceptually but left exact calculation depth unresolved.

This is therefore not a new concept but evidence that the reusable foundation needs a fuller time-value facet capable of being projected to courses that require it.

Future candidate content should distinguish:

- undiscounted cash flows versus present value;
- discount factor / discount rate meaning;
- NPV calculation from provided data at suitable Level 3 depth;
- interpretation of positive / negative NPV;
- comparison with payback and ARR;
- sensitivity to discount rate and cash-flow assumptions;
- non-financial factors;
- distinction between subject understanding and board-specific formula presentation.

### D-007 — Cost-benefit analysis

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed nodes:** `BUS-EVI-007` Whole-business judgement, trade-offs and strategic consistency; related `BUS-EVI-004` quantitative decision tools  
**Evidence:** Eduqas/WJEC explicitly include cost-benefit analysis.

The sealed foundation already teaches comparison of alternatives, costs, benefits, risk and uncertainty, but does not identify cost-benefit analysis as a distinct reusable decision method.

Future candidate content should cover:

- relevant costs and benefits;
- financial and non-financial impacts;
- time horizon;
- stakeholder distribution;
- monetisation limits;
- uncertainty / optimism bias;
- opportunity cost;
- sensitivity;
- why a positive quantified result cannot automatically decide the action.

### D-008 — International trade institutions / protectionism / FDI / market-entry methods

**Classification:** mixed:

- `GENUINE_SUBJECT_FOUNDATION_GAP` for protectionism / trade-policy mechanisms if retained as an A-level-wide subject capability;
- `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION` for FDI and international market-entry methods under `BUS-EXT-003`;
- `COURSE_SPECIFIC_SCOPE_OR_TERMINOLOGY` for named strategic frameworks that particular boards require.

**Evidence:** Pearson Edexcel explicitly includes FDI, protectionism and trading blocs; CCEA includes FDI and market-entry methods such as franchising, licensing, joint ventures and direct investment.

The sealed globalisation node is accurate but too broad to support all major qualifications without further facets.

Future candidate content should teach:

- reasons for international trade and international expansion;
- importing / exporting mechanisms;
- FDI;
- licensing, franchising, joint ventures and direct investment as entry choices;
- tariffs, quotas and subsidies;
- trading blocs / reduced trade barriers at an appropriate level;
- effects on costs, demand, competition, supply chain, exchange-rate exposure and strategic control;
- evaluation by context rather than generic claims that globalisation is good/bad.

### D-009 — Macroeconomic policy mechanisms

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-EXT-001` Growth, inflation, interest rates and labour-market conditions  
**Evidence:** CCEA explicitly extends the external environment into government macroeconomic objectives and policy instruments, including taxation, expenditure, interest rates and exchange rates.

The sealed foundation covers macroeconomic conditions and transmission, but not a sufficiently structured policy facet.

Future candidate content should cover fiscal / monetary policy at the level needed to explain business transmission, without turning the foundation into an Economics course.

### D-010 — Shareholder-specific ratios (EPS / ROE)

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-FIN-012` Profitability ratios and ROCE  
**Evidence:** CCEA explicitly requires EPS and ROE.

These are valid Business measures, but they are not universally required across the major English/Welsh specifications sampled. They should therefore be stored as optional subject facets that an exact course can select rather than used to redefine the common core.

### D-011 — Confidence intervals in marketing data

**Classification:** `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected sealed nodes:** `BUS-MKT-004` Sampling, bias and representativeness; `BUS-EVI-005` quantitative/qualitative data interpretation  
**Evidence:** AQA 7132 explicitly requires understanding the concept of confidence intervals in marketing data.

The sealed foundation teaches sampling error, bias and representativeness but does not explicitly teach confidence intervals. Add a bounded conceptual facet suitable for Business learners; advanced inferential-statistics mathematics remains out of scope unless an exact course requires more.

### D-012 — Contemporary people / sustainability / governance facets

**Classification:** predominantly `SUBJECT_DEPTH_EXTENSION`  
**Affected nodes:** people, supply chain, ethics and sustainability nodes  
**Evidence:** AQA 7138 and Cambridge OCR H436 explicitly increase visibility of EDIB, wellbeing/flexible work, supply-chain transparency/traceability, ESG reporting and contemporary sustainability issues.

The sealed foundation already contains recruitment, development, engagement, employee voice, ethics, CSR, sustainability, supply chains, net zero and environmental claims. The board challenge therefore does not justify new top-level nodes for these areas, but it does justify explicit facets for:

- equality, diversity, inclusion and belonging as organisational/workforce issues;
- employee wellbeing and flexible/hybrid work;
- talent acquisition / management / development as an integrated people capability;
- supply-chain transparency, traceability, responsible sourcing and audit mechanisms;
- ESG measures/reporting and the distinction between measurement, disclosure and genuine performance.

### D-013 — AI and disruptive technology

**Classification:** `EXISTING_FOUNDATION_SUFFICIENT` at subject-foundation level  
**Affected sealed node:** `BUS-STR-008` Innovation, R&D, IP and digital transformation  
**Evidence:** AQA 7138 explicitly names disruptive technology including AI; Cambridge OCR H436 includes AI within technology in marketing.

The sealed foundation already teaches digital transformation as a capability/process issue, technology adoption, skills, data, process redesign and implementation. AI-specific examples can be attached without inventing a separate fundamental Business node unless future assurance shows a genuinely distinct educational obligation.

### D-014 — Named frameworks and theorists

**Classification:** primarily `COURSE_SPECIFIC_SCOPE_OR_TERMINOLOGY`  
**Evidence:** Different boards require different named frameworks, for example Handy, Kotter/Schlesinger, Balanced Scorecard, Triple Bottom Line, Carroll, Porter Generic Strategy, Bowman, and varying motivation theories.

These differences validate the Phase 1 decision to separate models/frameworks from underlying factual subject truth. The reusable models register may expand, but a board requiring a named model does not by itself prove the Business subject taxonomy was incomplete.

## Summary of board challenge

The post-seal breadth challenge produced:

- **1 clear new reusable method/node candidate:** network analysis / Critical Path Analysis;
- **1 likely genuine international-business subject gap:** protectionism / trade-policy mechanics, best implemented as a facet or tightly scoped node after assurance;
- **multiple depth/facet extensions** to already-correct nodes: YED, sales forecasting methods, customer journey/CRM, workforce metrics, NPV depth, cost-benefit analysis, international entry/FDI, macro policy, shareholder ratios, confidence intervals, EDIB/wellbeing/ESG/supply-chain transparency;
- **modern examples but no new underlying subject truth:** AI/disruptive technology;
- **board-specific model requirements rather than factual gaps:** named frameworks/theorists.

The challenge therefore supports the architecture's reuse hypothesis: most awarding-body requirements map to the sealed independent Business universe, and the gaps can be handled by bounded subject-foundation deltas rather than recreating Business per board.

## Important limitation

This challenge establishes breadth deltas; it is not yet the exact Specification Mapping / Course Truth for any individual qualification. Each exact course still needs complete requirement-by-requirement mapping, depth/capability resolution and Exam Truth under Revision's governed Content Factory process.
