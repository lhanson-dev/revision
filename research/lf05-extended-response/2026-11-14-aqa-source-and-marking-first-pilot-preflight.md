## AQA-derived criteria used for this bounded review

1. **Specification fit:** named theory, technique or skill really occurs in the **applicable version's** exact required/optional topic and supported paper; Psychology P3 option groups respected.
2. **Format:** substantive essay/question demand broad enough to warrant the displayed long-form training length; Psychology contextual application must have original relevant hooks, not a distractingly long fictional case. AQA spec does **not** say every narrow Psychology topic demand is a 16-marker.
3. **Assessment-objective integrity:** for Psychology AO1 knowledge / AO2 application / AO3 analysis and evaluation; for Business AO1 terms / AO2 contextual application / AO3 causal analysis / AO4 judgement. Overall AO total must equal the draft tariff but that **does not** verify the scheme distribution.
4. **Support quality:** after first response only, use question-specific accurate indicative points, meaningful evidence and counterargument, legitimate alternative reasoning, holistic levels and calibrated uncertainty. Do not promise one mark per checklist bullet or that the student's own score is independently graded.
5. **Source permissions:** specifications and historical AQA examples are `REFERENCE_ONLY` for alignment; do not copy protected original questions, AQA cases or third-party marking grids into the Revision authored bank.
