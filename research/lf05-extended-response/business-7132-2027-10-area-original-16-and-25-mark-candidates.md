## Provisional original Revision level guides (do not call these AQA official 2027 mark schemes)

**16-mark case-evaluation model** (illustrative AO1 2, AO2 3, AO3 4, AO4 7; check actual specific tariff/AOs before releasing):
- Level 4 **13–16**: accurate selected knowledge, highly developed linked application to the *given business*, well-reasoned alternative impacts and a balanced evidence-based final judgement.
- Level 3 **9–12**: generally relevant explanation, developed but uneven contextual chains, useful counterargument and some justified judgement.
- Level 2 **5–8**: partial development, occasional case references, one-sided or weakly linked judgement.
- Level 1 **1–4**: fragmentary knowledge, description or assertion; little real evaluation. **0** for no relevant content.

**25-mark Paper 1 holistic essay model** (illustrative AO1 5, AO2 4, AO3 6, AO4 10; same item-level revalidation rule):
- Level 5 **21–25**: wide and precise selected knowledge, well-developed analysis, effective contextual application and sustained balanced judgement explicitly answering the whole question.
- Level 4 **16–20**: good knowledge and developed argument with relevant context, balance and a justified but less sophisticated overall judgement.
- Level 3 **11–15**: some developed reasoning and context; balance or whole-question judgement uneven.
- Level 2 **6–10**: partial knowledge, mainly descriptive or superficial analysis and limited focus/judgement.
- Level 1 **1–5**: scattered limited points with little application or reasoning. **0** where no relevant content.

**Marking procedure:** read the learner's committed original answer before revealing content; choose overall level first, mark within band using accuracy, focus, relevance and development. The indicative points below are **not** a one-point-equals-one-mark checklist. Defensible alternative arguments must be credited. All written results are labelled **learner self-assessed**, never independently verified marks or mastery; subsequent attempts have different evidence IDs and original answer remains immutable.

**Paper constraints:** all 10 broad content areas can be examined in any of the 7132 papers, but the existence of a 16/25-mark *candidate* for each area is **not** proof that the board will ask those particular prompts or use these AO splits. 25-mark choices belong only to Paper 1 essay format; 16-mark responses should preserve Paper 2 or 3-style source and paper/section link. A paper-level case with related questions must be internally coherent and not inadvertently leak answers.
