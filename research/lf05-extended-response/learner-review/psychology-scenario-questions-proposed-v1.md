## PSY-LF05-01-S1 — Social influence, contextual extended writing

**Course/source alignment candidate:** AQA A-level Psychology 7182, v1.4 (2027), §4.1.1, resistance to social influence (social support and locus of control). Paper 1 topic; proposed **16-mark-style training question**. **AO allocation and tariff unapproved.**

**Original fictional scenario:** At a sixth-form council meeting, seven student representatives are asked by the college organiser to support a proposed timetable change. Leila privately thinks the change is unfair. At first she stays silent while other representatives agree. When another student openly objects, Leila says that she also disagrees and explains why. Later Leila says that she prefers to make decisions based on her own judgement rather than simply following the group.

**Question:** Discuss explanations for resistance to social influence, using the situation above in your answer.

## PSY-LF05-02-S1 — Memory, contextual extended writing

**Course/source alignment candidate:** AQA A-level Psychology 7182, v1.4 (2027), §4.1.2, working-memory model (central executive, phonological loop, visuo-spatial sketchpad and episodic buffer). Paper 1 topic; proposed **16-mark-style training question**. **AO allocation and tariff unapproved.**

**Original fictional scenario and illustrative results:** A researcher asks 24 sixth-form volunteers to remember six spoken digits while they complete a second task. In Task A, they track the changing positions of coloured shapes on a screen. In Task B, they repeat a sequence of spoken words. Average accuracy on the digit task is **76% in Task A** and **49% in Task B**. Everyone completes Task A before Task B. The second tasks were not independently matched for difficulty. These figures are invented for the question; they are **not real research findings**.

**Question:** Discuss how useful the working-memory model is for explaining the results of this investigation.
