# Draft long-answer guidance — learner review v1

Open this only **after** making a first attempt or outline. This is Revision-authored indicative guidance for review, not an official AQA mark scheme and not an examiner mark. It deliberately gives no score for a learner's work.

## PSY-LF05-01 — Social influence

A useful response may:

- explain resistance to conformity and obedience, social support and internal/external locus of control;
- use relevant evidence carefully, including limits on what controlled settings show;
- compare situational support with dispositional control rather than treating either factor as complete;
- reach a reasoned conclusion about when each explanation is helpful.

Other accurate, relevant reasoning deserves credit. Simply listing studies or terminology would not by itself answer the command.

## PSY-LF05-02 — Memory

A useful response may:

- explain the central executive, phonological loop, visuo-spatial sketchpad and episodic buffer;
- connect separate components to doing different short-term tasks at the same time;
- use dual-task findings and consider limits on the central executive or laboratory/case evidence;
- reach a judgement about the working-memory model's explanatory value and limits.

Relevant cognitive-task or brain-research evidence can be useful when it is explained, not merely named.

## PSY-LF05-08 — Issues and debates

A useful response may:

- distinguish biological/heritable and environmental influences, while explaining interactionism;
- use accurate examples from topics the learner has actually studied;
- question strict nature-only or nurture-only explanations and consider implications of gene–environment interaction;
- make a synoptic judgement based on the quality and relevance of the examples, rather than on a fixed number of named studies.

Examples from compulsory topics are always available; optional-topic examples are valid only where that option has actually been studied.

## BUS-LF05-05-16 — Financial management

A useful response may:

- distinguish borrowing costs, liquidity, profitability and sources of finance;
- use the case figures: 8% operating profit margin, a 0.875:1 current ratio, negative working capital, the £1m requirement and seasonal orders;
- weigh access to finance and retained ownership against interest, collateral and cash-flow risk;
- compare alternatives such as retained profit, equity or leasing;
- make a conditional recommendation based on forecast cash flow, debt service and cost of capital.

A different recommendation can be strong if it follows from well-explained case evidence.

## BUS-LF05-09-16 — Strategic methods

A useful response may:

- distinguish acquisition/external growth from organic growth and explain possible synergy;
- use the £8m versus £5m cost, 240 drivers, incompatible IT, disputed contracts and 18-month ambition;
- weigh speed and capacity gains against integration, debt, contract and management risks;
- compare the execution time and predictability of opening depots;
- reach a judgement about whether the speed premium is worth the integration risk.

A strong answer needs a reasoned judgement, not simply a list of acquisition advantages and disadvantages.

## BUS-LF05-03-25 — Marketing

A useful response may:

- explain relevant marketing-research, segmentation, targeting, positioning and marketing-mix ideas;
- consider how information quality can reduce uncertainty but still be limited by sampling, extrapolation and reliability;
- analyse how product, price, place and promotion can succeed or fail even when research is sound;
- compare circumstances such as a new versus established market and different launch budgets;
- reach a comparative, qualified conclusion about whether information quality or execution is the more important constraint.

A defensible alternative conclusion is valid when it is supported by coherent business reasoning.

## BUS-LF05-05-25 — Financial management

A useful response may:

- distinguish liquidity/cash flow from profitability and relevant financing decisions;
- explain why rapid sales growth can consume working capital;
- compare improved margins/pricing with cash-conversion, inventory and credit-control measures;
- judge when liquidity is the immediate survival constraint and when profitability supports sustainable finance;
- reach a conclusion that accounts for the business model and credit terms.

A good answer does not assume that profit and cash flow always point to the same decision.
