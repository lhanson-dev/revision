## BUS-LF05-05-16 — Financial management (16-mark-style contextual evaluation)

**Read only after answering the original Harbour Furniture case question.** The original stimulus, with operating profit £480,000, sales £6m, current assets £700,000, current liabilities £800,000, a proposed £1m expansion and seasonal orders, must stay attached to that question. Do not infer extra loan, margin or repayments data that the prompt did not provide.

**Core task:** evaluate whether a **long-term bank loan** is suitable financing for Harbour Furniture's expansion, using its actual financial condition and conditional alternatives.

**Proposed AOs for *review* (unverified as an item-level 2027 profile):** AO1 2 (finance knowledge); AO2 3 (application); AO3 4 (developed causal analysis); AO4 7 (evaluation/judgement).

- **AO1 / correct concepts:** Bank loan as borrowed long-term capital with interest and repayment obligations; potential secured collateral and variable-rate exposure; distinguish liquidity/working capital, profit and sources of finance.
- **AO2 / case-specific reasoning:** Operating margin = £480,000 ÷ £6,000,000 ×100 = **8%**; current ratio = £700,000 ÷ £800,000 = **0.875:1**; current assets are **£100,000 below** current liabilities. The firm is reported profitable, but low immediate liquidity and seasonality may affect debt service. Ratios alone do not prove insolvency, that a loan would be rejected or that expansion fails.
- **AO3 / linked analysis:** Explain a credible chain such as new borrowing → financed capacity/market opportunity → potentially higher sales; versus seasonal cash inflows + variable interest + existing negative net working capital → pressure on debt servicing and potentially suppliers. Separate a one-off investment funding decision from ongoing working-capital needs.
- **AO4 / alternatives and judgement:** A defensible **yes** might require cash-flow forecasts, repayment cover, collateral resilience and reliable future demand; a defensible **no** could favour phased expansion, leasing relevant assets, retained earnings if sufficient, equity if suitable to ownership or a smaller financing mix. Evaluate feasibility, not merely list options. End with a reasoned recommendation that takes the **£1m requirement**, security, repayment risk and growth case into account.

**Strong response:** Accurate finance concepts; selective calculations interpreted rather than merely displayed; two connected competing consequences; feasible alternatives; clear conditional recommendation tied to case facts.

**Developing response:** Some accurate figures or finance terminology, but only one developed causal chain, little assessment of alternatives or unclear reasoning from figures to judgement.

**Weak response:** States “loans are bad because debt” or “profits mean they can afford it” without evidence; mistakes £480,000 profit for cash or treats a current ratio below 1 as proof of bankruptcy.

**Permitted alternative conclusions:** A convincing conditional pro-loan judgement or a well-supported alternative-finance judgement can both be valuable. Do not require one predetermined answer.

**Post-attempt reflection, not an examiner score:** Which one financial fact most changed your judgement, why, and what future information would change it? Pick one improvement for your *next* independent answer. A self-awarded number is **not independently verified**.

## BUS-LF05-03-25 — Marketing (25-mark-style Paper 1 extended essay)

**Read only after answering:** “To what extent is accurate marketing research more important than an effective marketing mix when launching a new product?” There is **no supplied case** here by design: Business 7132 Paper 1 Section C/D can ask broad essay questions. Candidate business contexts must be internally coherent rather than fabricated as official scenarios.

**Proposed AOs for *review* (unverified item-specific 2027 split):** AO1 5 (knowledge); AO2 4 (credible contextual application); AO3 6 (developed analysis); AO4 10 (sustained evaluation). **Provisional holistic levels, not per-bullet point scoring.**

- **AO1 / correct concepts:** Define fit-for-purpose marketing research, market information reliability, sampling, segmentation/targeting and an effective integrated marketing mix (product, price, promotion, place and the wider 7Ps where context warrants).
- **AO2 / relevant application:** Build contrasting **plausible** launch situations—e.g., a new specialist niche with uncertain demand versus a familiar repeat-purchase market with reliable customer insight. Context must illustrate how research and the actual mix interact, not repeat brand names as decoration.
- **AO3 / developed analysis:** Explain how accurate research can reduce demand uncertainty and inform product/price decisions; why even accurate insight cannot rescue an unaffordable price or unavailable distribution; and how a compelling mix may fail when market research ignores representative buyers. Demonstrate **interdependence**, not only two separate lists.
- **AO4 / sustained judgement:** Weigh when accurate research matters more than execution, when execution is decisive, and why product type, competition, decision speed, budget, sampling quality and launch uncertainty alter the judgement. Finish with a prioritised, qualified answer to “more important”, not a generic recommendation to “do both”.

**Strong response:** Comparative line of reasoning maintained throughout with meaningful contextual examples, robust counterarguments and a final prioritisation that states **under which conditions** research or mix is more important.

**Developing response:** Relevant terms and examples but analysis is unbalanced; evaluation appears only in a short unsupported conclusion or treats “marketing mix” as promotion alone.

**Weak response:** Definitional lists, assertions that research “always works”, or an unrelated SWOT essay without explaining how either factor influences a launch.

**Permitted alternative conclusions:** Either research-first or marketing-mix-first, or an interaction-led judgement, may be defensible when the comparison is explained. The mark demand is based on the **quality of reasoning and judgement**, not which side is selected.

**Post-attempt reflection, not an examiner score:** Underline the sentence where you answer “more important”. Identify the strongest counterargument and improve the link between a specific launch context and your final condition.

## Training-only common quality bands for discussion, NOT an approved numerical rubric

| Broad quality band | What the question-specific response shows |
| --- | --- |
| Strong | Accurate selection, relevant application, linked analysis and genuinely sustained evaluative judgement on the comparative command |
| Developing | Mostly accurate and relevant with some strong chains, but uneven alternative treatment and limited final prioritisation |
| Partial | Some sound concepts and examples; mostly descriptive, underdeveloped and only loosely evaluative |
| Limited | Isolated assertions, inaccurate interpretation, irrelevant context or no meaningful judgement |
