# LF-05 learner review pack — current candidate wording

**Purpose:** a private, unmarked usability and face-validity review of seven original Revision long-answer *drafts*. This is not a live Revision feature, an AQA paper, an official AQA mark scheme, a prediction of exam performance, or independent educational approval.

**Status:** research only. All seven items remain unpublished and subject to source/tariff, independent subject/assessment, integration and learner-acceptance gates. In particular, the Psychology items are 16-mark-*style* training candidates: their 2027 item-level tariff eligibility has not been established.

## Version control

- **Pack version:** `learner-review-v1-2026-11-12`
- **Source candidate files:** `psychology-7182-2027-17-topic-original-16-mark-candidates.md` and `business-7132-2027-10-area-original-16-and-25-mark-candidates.md`
- **Supersedes:** the earlier external seven-item learner pack for `PSY-LF05-02` and `PSY-LF05-08`. Do not treat feedback on the old wording as feedback on the current Memory or Issues and Debates prompts.
- **No status change:** this pack does not make any item approved, published, exam-authentic, examiner-marked or ready for product implementation.

## How to use the pack

1. Share **only** [questions-current-v1.md](questions-current-v1.md) first.
2. Ask the learner to attempt one question, or make a clear outline, before looking at any guidance. A private draft is enough; there is no need to share it.
3. Then share [guidance-current-v1.md](guidance-current-v1.md).
4. Collect observations using [feedback-sheet-current-v1.md](feedback-sheet-current-v1.md).

Please do **not** post a learner's full answer, name, school, credentials or other personal data in GitHub. Record only the item ID, the review observations and any requested revision. The review is about clarity, realism, context and usefulness—not grading the learner.

## What happens after feedback

Record the feedback against the immutable item IDs. Any material wording, stimulus or guidance change requires a fresh current-version review. This learner review does not replace independent A1/A2/A3/A4 review, LF-05 human Ready, durable draft/reveal/evidence design, production assurance or post-release learner acceptance.
