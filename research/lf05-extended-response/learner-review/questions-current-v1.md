## Business

### BUS-LF05-05-16 — Financial management

Harbour Furniture Ltd reports operating profit of £480,000 on sales of £6m, current assets of £700,000 and current liabilities of £800,000. A £1m expansion could be financed by a secured loan at a variable rate, but orders are seasonal.

Evaluate whether a long-term bank loan is a suitable way for Harbour Furniture to finance its expansion.

**[16 marks — draft training candidate]**

### BUS-LF05-09-16 — Strategic methods

Briar Logistics Ltd can acquire a regional competitor for £8m or open three depots organically for £5m. The acquisition adds 240 drivers but has incompatible IT systems and two disputed contracts. Managers want a national network within 18 months.

Evaluate whether acquiring the competitor is a better way for Briar Logistics to grow than opening depots organically.

**[16 marks — draft training candidate]**

### BUS-LF05-03-25 — Marketing

To what extent is accurate marketing research more important than an effective marketing mix when launching a new product?

**[25 marks — draft training candidate]**

### BUS-LF05-05-25 — Financial management

To what extent should improving cash flow take priority over increasing profitability when a business is experiencing rapid growth?

**[25 marks — draft training candidate]**
