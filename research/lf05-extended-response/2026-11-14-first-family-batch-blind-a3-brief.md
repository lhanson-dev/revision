## Independent reviewer task, stage A

For each item, **without seeing its authored indicative guidance**:

1. Evaluate precise stated specification/topic fit and wrong-paper/optional-topic risk; flag any concept missing from the updated cohort.
2. Judge whether the demand is legitimately substantial enough for the *displayed extended-writing training length*; distinguish this from evidence that AQA will set this tariff in 2027.
