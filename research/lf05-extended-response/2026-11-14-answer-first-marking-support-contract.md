## One canonical response journey (no new marking engine)

1. **Choose:** authenticated learner → canonical `/revision/app/` → Course/topic Practice OR Exam Prep paper/marks selection → **only source-assured, published, exact applicable** question IDs. Label Revision-authored and `extended-writing training` when an authentic 2027 tariff is not demonstrated. Paper 3 chosen options remain bounded.
2. **Question before answer:** display exact stem, marks, command, source/section/option and all **necessary, original stimulus** in the *same accessible view*. For Business Paper 2/3-style 16 marks this requires the question's actual named-business case and information. Business Paper 1's broad 25-mark essays legitimately have no supplied case. Psychology AO1/AO3 evaluations can be standalone; an AO2 contextual version must include enough relevant stimulus hooks and appropriate AO allocation. Do not hide cases in separate guides or invent a scenario simply to fill a quota.
3. **Draft safely:** substantial text entry, explicit “Saved” vs “Saving / could not save” truth where an **approved owner-private durable draft** is available; recovery must preserve the exact question and version. No raw answer logging to third-party analytics and no reliance on timed mock draft retention as implied permission for untimed writing. Resolve retention, expiry, deletion, owner RLS and child-data activation before real pilot use.
4. **Explicit first attempt:** learner clicks **Submit my answer** after writing. The first response is safely confirmed and versioned/immutable before marking guide, checklist, model or exemplar can be shown. A whitespace or token answer is **not** evidence of substantial independent performance. Handle zero/invalid/non-submission as unscored; do not lose a genuine raw draft after a failed commit. No silent post-reveal editing of the original.
5. **After verified commit only:** show **Revision's marking guidance** in these distinct groups: (a) what this **specific** question tests, including source skill(s) and proposed AO; (b) **indicative content** tied to the actual command/context/data; (c) acceptable **alternative explanations or conclusions**; (d) **holistic level-of-response descriptions** for breadth, reasoning and evidence, distinguishing strong/partial/limited responses; (e) **common pitfalls**, not an exhaustive one-bullet-per-mark checklist; (f) one actionable **next improvement** and a link to a **different** source-assured retest item. Reveal guide only for the question/version just submitted, never from one topic's generic checklist.
6. **Self-assess honestly:** clearly label **Self-assessed; not AQA, an examiner, or independent AI marking.** A learner may reflect and optionally provide self-awarded AO observations/marks if the individual scheme is independently reviewed and permits that. Do not compute a pseudo-precise verified score from mere guide viewing, pre-fill a mark, inflate mastery, or attribute a multi-skill score to every mapped skill. Preserve historical 0 scores and previous attempts. Revisions and retakes require new identities.
7. **Retest:** only a different independently valid question without prior guide exposure can provide a fresh attempt; a self-assessment can prompt better practice but is not a scored independent repair/recheck on #703. Keep existing Home/Plan/REV evidence and autonomy boundaries.
