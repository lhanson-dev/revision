## Authenticity checklist — every item must pass independently

1. **A1 source/legal:** compare the exact 2027 source material (not Psychology 2014/2020 and not Business 7138). Record source version, section/page, official sample/2027 Set 3 access status, provenance and rights. Distinguish exact applicable course from training analogue. No copying AQA text, cases or entire mark schemes.
2. **AQA paper reality:** does the item actually belong in this *paper*, and can this breadth/command validly support the proposed marks? Psychology Paper 3 options respected; Business Paper 1 **two 25-mark choice sections**, 7132 Paper 2 data groups, Paper 3 integrated case. Section/paper marks must remain accurate; isolated writing drills remain *practice-only* and never inserted as compulsory questions into a fixed mock.
3. **Independent A2 subject knowledge:** every scientific/business assertion, empirical generalisation, number, alternative answer, case hook, terminology and current specification noun independently checked. Identify overspecific or missing content and correct rather than inventing.
4. **Independent blinded A3 assessment:** examine question **before reading the authored indicative content/answer key**. Verify command, ambiguity, AO and mark distribution, genuine 16/25 mark demand, discriminatory levels, contextual relevance, fairness, realistic time, original material, complete level criteria and creditable alternatives. Reject forced 16s on narrow psychological material.
5. **Response calibration:** use **new original** high/middle/low exemplar responses for each item with rationale; ideally qualified subject-teacher/examiner review. An LLM reviewing its own prompt with the answer key exposed does **not** establish independent marking accuracy. Compare levels for different defensible answers and incorrect/missing components, not superficial checklist coverage.
6. **Independent A4 integration:** select via enrolled Course → topic → validated mark, and via Exam Prep → paper → valid mark; confirm stimulus/section/command/mark always travel with the item, written response can be **committed first**, draft autosaves/reloads with owner RLS, marking guidance appears only *after* commit, saved zero/historical answers persist, and self-awarded marks cannot become independent mastery or overwhelm objective evidence.
7. **Fresh variant:** to support learning improvement, draft at least **two distinct fully reviewed questions per eligible substantial topic** before claiming useful repeated coverage. Do not show the same model and then re-present a cosmetic paraphrase as a fresh independent test.
8. **Recorded disposition:** `item_id`, immutable item + guide versions, precise source URL/page, qualification and 2027 applicability, required/optional topic choice, paper, reason for tariff, source-specific AO/level, independent A2 reviewer/result, independent A3 reviewer/result, A4 proof, `exam_authentic | training_only | unavailable`, whether *actual* learner content is published, review date, and specific next issue/PR. Denials and HOLDs remain visible.
9. **Safe product release:** LF-05 must first meet **all 17 Definition-of-Ready** conditions including normative authority/retention and explicit human Ready; code/content branches then undergo required CI, auth/database/responsive/device/accessibility tests and **specific Founder PR merge approval**. Production deployment evidence and original learner acceptance are later gates, not implied by merged code.
