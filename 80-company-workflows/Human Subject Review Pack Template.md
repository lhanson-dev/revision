# Human Subject Review Pack Template

## Purpose
Provide a qualified human subject reviewer with one complete, repeatable review package for a Revision content pack before that pack is treated as a commercial teaching benchmark.

This template is part of the Content Pack Production and Assurance Workflow and the Content Accuracy Assurance Gate.

## Pack structure
Each human-review package should contain or reference:

1. **Reviewer brief** — exact course identity, review objective, what the reviewer should and should not assume, and the required decision.
2. **Revision content inventory** — every learner-facing content file included in the reviewed pack, by exact repository path and reviewed commit.
3. **Official source register** — current primary awarding-body sources and what each source governs. Link to official material rather than copying substantial copyrighted text into Revision.
4. **Coverage record** — the specification-to-Revision mapping and any intentionally deferred areas.
5. **Prior assurance record** — automated, source-led, arithmetic and adversarial checks already performed, including resolved issues and remaining limitations.
6. **Human review checklist** — factual accuracy, curriculum scope, pedagogy, assessment authenticity, marking guidance and harmful/misleading simplification.
7. **Issue register** — item ID, severity, finding, source/evidence, recommended correction and resolution status.
8. **Reviewer sign-off** — reviewer identity/qualification, date, scope actually reviewed and PASS / CONDITIONAL PASS / FAIL decision.

## Reviewer instructions
The reviewer is not being asked to rewrite the pack or improve its style. The primary task is to identify educational risk.

Review against the exact stated qualification/specification/component. Use official awarding-body material as authority. Do not approve material because it is generally true in the subject if it is outside the learner's specification or could teach the wrong exam behaviour.

Reviewers should actively look for:

- incorrect facts, definitions, formulas or interpretations;
- content that is technically true but outside the stated specification and therefore misleadingly prioritised;
- oversimplifications that create a false rule;
- missing qualification/conditions in explanations;
- inaccurate or implausible worked examples;
- incorrect numerical answers, units or formula use;
- misleading flashcard answers or MCQ distractors;
- exam-style questions that assess the wrong concept or level of demand;
- case facts that are internally inconsistent or implausible enough to distort the answer;
- marking guidance that rewards invalid reasoning or claims to reproduce an official mark scheme when it does not;
- poor alignment to assessment objectives, question command words, timing or paper structure;
- important specification gaps that would make the pack appear more complete than it is;
- common misconceptions that the current wording could reinforce.

Style, tone and minor copy-editing may be noted separately but should not obscure accuracy findings.

## Sampling versus full review
For a benchmark pack, the reviewer should inspect the full topic/learning coverage and all substantial assessment assets. High-volume atomic items such as flashcards or MCQs may use a documented risk-based sampling approach only if the reviewer is satisfied that:

- every specification area is represented in the sample;
- all formulas and quantitative items are checked;
- any item class with a discovered material defect is escalated to full review;
- the sampling method and sample size are recorded.

Full simulations, substantial cases, exam technique, model/marking guidance and coverage records should not be sampled: review all of them.

## Severity model
- **Blocking** — content should not remain learner-facing; likely to teach a materially wrong fact, rule, method, curriculum requirement or exam behaviour.
- **Material** — significant inaccuracy, omission, scope drift or assessment problem that must be corrected before commercial benchmark approval.
- **Minor** — low-risk wording/precision issue that does not materially misteach but should be corrected.
- **No issue** — checked and acceptable.

## Human decision
- **PASS** — no unresolved blocking/material findings; reviewer considers the pack factually sound, in-scope and educationally suitable for the stated learner/course.
- **CONDITIONAL PASS** — suitable only with explicitly documented non-critical limitations and a defined remediation/revalidation action.
- **FAIL / HOLD** — unresolved blocking/material findings, missing source authority, uncertain course identity, or review scope insufficient to support approval.

A human PASS does not mean Revision may claim zero errors. It means the benchmark pack has completed the governed human subject-review control.

## Required sign-off fields
- Reviewer name
- Relevant role/qualification/experience
- Organisation (optional)
- Conflict/relationship to Revision, if any
- Date reviewed
- Exact repository commit/version reviewed
- Review scope completed
- Sampling approach, if any
- Blocking findings count
- Material findings count
- Minor findings count
- Final decision
- Conditions/remediation required
- Reviewer statement: `I reviewed this material against the stated specification and official sources to the scope recorded above.`
