# Content Factory Independent Subject Research Baseline Amendment

**Status:** Active on Founder-approved merge  
**Decision date:** 27 September 2026  
**Owner:** Founder / Product / Educational Content / Content Operations  
**Amends:** `Content Factory Subject Knowledge Foundation and Course Projection Amendment.md`  
**Decision record:** `decisions/ADR-0029-independent-subject-research-before-content-reuse.md`

## Purpose

Prevent existing exam-board-shaped or previously generated course content from silently defining a reusable Subject Knowledge Foundation.

This amendment adds a mandatory **fresh independent subject-research baseline** between the existing-artifact inventory and promotion/reconciliation of prior course content.

Where this amendment conflicts with the sequencing or research threshold in the parent Subject Knowledge Foundation amendment or current Business trial technical contract, this amendment governs the specific rules below.

## Governing rule

> **Existing course-derived educational content may not become reusable Subject Knowledge Foundation truth merely because it already exists, passed an exact-course check, or has prior assurance. A fresh independent subject-research baseline must first establish the subject universe against which existing content is reconciled.**

The purpose is to prevent:

- anchoring the reusable subject model to the first exam board;
- carrying forward omissions that earlier Course Truth did not detect;
- confusing exact-course completeness with subject-family completeness;
- overfitting the subject taxonomy to a specification's organisation; and
- repeatedly expanding the foundation only after later boards expose predictable gaps.

## Revised first-course sequence

For the first governed course in a new subject family, the sequence is:

`T1 existing-artifact inventory → T2 sealed independent subject-research baseline → T3 post-seal cross-board breadth challenge → T4 reconcile prior content against baseline → T5 Subject Knowledge Foundation candidate + assurance → exact specification mapping/gap reconciliation → exact Course Truth + Exam Truth → exact-course assurance → Course Learning Blueprint → assets`

For a genuinely new subject with no existing content estate, T1 may be trivial, but T2 remains mandatory.

For later courses in an already-assured subject family, the full independent baseline is not rerun unless a material defect, scope expansion or revalidation trigger makes it necessary. Normal later-course work remains mapping + delta reconciliation against the retained Subject Knowledge Foundation.

## Independence boundary

### Phase 1 — sealed subject baseline

The research context establishing the initial subject baseline must not inspect or use as its starting material:

- Revision's existing Course Truth / CKM;
- current subject source seeds created for an exact course;
- Revision Learn, Practice or Exam Prep content;
- earlier generation/remediation findings;
- Revision's existing subject taxonomy or node IDs; or
- exam-board specifications as the organising outline.

The researcher may know the intended subject family and qualification level boundary, for example `UK Level 3 / A-level Business`, because scope must be bounded.

The baseline must be created from fresh permitted external subject evidence and sealed before exam-board comparison begins.

### Phase 2 — breadth challenge only after sealing

After the independent baseline is frozen, current major relevant exam-board specifications may be researched through official sources within the Source Licensing and Provenance Standard.

Their role is to challenge breadth and depth, not to become generative source prose or silently redefine generic Subject Truth.

Any new apparent requirement must first be classified as:

- genuine reusable subject gap;
- reusable depth/facet extension;
- exact-course scope/terminology;
- Exam Truth only; or
- unsupported/out of scope.

The sealed baseline and the post-board delta register must remain separately visible so the process can show what was independently discovered versus what an exam-board comparison added.

## Reconciliation rule

Only after the independent baseline and breadth challenge exist may Revision reconcile prior content.

Each existing subject/course knowledge object must be classified at the smallest safe scope as one or more of:

- `REUSE_ALIGNED`;
- `EXPAND_TO_BASELINE`;
- `SPLIT_FOR_GRANULARITY`;
- `MERGE_DUPLICATE`;
- `CORRECT`;
- `COURSE_SPECIFIC`;
- `INSUFFICIENT_PROVENANCE`;
- `RESEARCH_CONFLICT`;
- `REJECT`; or
- `NEEDS_SPECIALIST_JUDGEMENT`.

Previous assurance can be retained where its evidence still applies, but a changed representation must not inherit assurance beyond the claims/dependencies actually reviewed.

## Completeness standard for the independent baseline

The initial subject research must use multiple independent source families and include:

1. an initial subject-universe pass;
2. a deliberate gap-hunt using different source taxonomies/authorities;
3. a saturation challenge showing whether further suitable source families continue to expose material Level 3/A-level nodes;
4. quantitative/method completeness where applicable;
5. misconceptions, conditions, limitations and relationships;
6. real-world application/transfer rather than exam-only terminology; and
7. an explicit residual-uncertainty register.

Do not claim an infinite or universal encyclopedia. The boundary remains the deliberately supported subject/qualification family.

## Source-rights rule

The existing Educational Content Source Licensing and Provenance Standard remains fully in force.

Fresh research is not permission to ingest protected material. Subject truth intended for reusable AI-assisted production must be independently established from permitted OPEN, REVISION_OWNED or appropriately LICENSED evidence.

Exam-board specifications used in the post-seal challenge remain subject to their approved structured/reference-only treatment unless broader rights are explicitly recorded.

## Assurance relationship

Independent subject research is an evidence input, not automatic subject approval.

After reconciliation, the resulting Subject Knowledge Foundation candidate must still pass its applicable source/factual, structural and independent educational assurance.

Exact-course Foundation assurance also remains mandatory. The subject baseline does not prove that a particular specification is fully mapped or that its Exam Truth is correct.

## Business trial application

For AQA A-level Business 7132:

1. continue T1 inventory without promoting current `BUS-7132-*` Course Truth into shared subject truth;
2. run the fresh independent Business research brief in `research/Business Subject Knowledge Independent Research Brief - 2026-09-27.md` in a genuinely fresh context;
3. seal that research baseline before exposing it to Revision's existing Business content or board specifications;
4. run the post-seal current-board breadth challenge;
5. reconcile the existing 49-node AQA CKM, source seed and other Business educational material against the independent baseline; and
6. build the Business Subject Knowledge Foundation candidate from the reconciled result rather than from the AQA taxonomy alone.

## Documentation impact

The Business technical trial must reflect this sequencing. `INDEX.md` must expose this amendment and the independent research brief so later agents cannot revert to an existing-content-first subject-foundation build.

Historical Foundation and asset evidence remains unchanged and is inventoried as evidence rather than rewritten.
