# Foundation Approval Pack Completeness Contract

**Status:** Draft implementation contract for PR #318  
**Date:** 5 September 2026  
**Authority:** `80-company-workflows/Content Factory Foundation Completeness Amendment.md`

## Approval-pack defect being corrected

The prior expert-review package contained the exact Foundation artifacts and assurance evidence, but not a first-class independent statement of the complete applicable curriculum/exam obligation universe.

That allowed a coherent but incomplete Foundation to reach human review.

## Required package contents

The qualified-human package for a Foundation must contain, at minimum:

1. exact Foundation Candidate identity and fingerprint;
2. Source Licence Register;
3. Board Alignment;
4. Foundation Requirement Baseline and reconciliation;
5. Foundation Coverage Model;
6. Course Knowledge Model / Course Truth;
7. Assessment Blueprint / Exam Truth;
8. applicable Question Families;
9. deterministic assurance evidence;
10. independent-review evidence; and
11. known limitations.

The Foundation Requirement Baseline is not optional when curriculum/specification completeness is being approved.

## Reviewer task

The reviewer must answer two separate questions:

1. **Baseline completeness:** Does the requirement baseline itself appear to represent the complete applicable course and exam requirements for this exact cohort, including relevant scope boundaries and removed/stale-content exclusions?
2. **Foundation satisfaction:** Does the supplied Course Truth and Exam Truth substantively satisfy every mapped material obligation?

A `pass` requires both questions to be satisfied with no blocking/material finding.

## Why both levels are required

Deterministic reconciliation can prove that every known baseline obligation maps somewhere. It cannot prove that the baseline omitted nothing.

Qualified subject/assessment review therefore remains necessary to challenge the completeness and correctness of the baseline itself.

Likewise, a human should not be forced to infer the full specification universe from a large Course Knowledge Model. The baseline makes completeness inspectable and auditable.

## Current AQA status

The retained AQA 7132 expert package is historical evidence and remains not approved. It must not be reused for approval after this correction because it does not contain the new Foundation Requirement Baseline.

A new Foundation fingerprint and a new qualified-human package are required after the AQA baseline and remediated Foundation pass deterministic and independent assurance.
