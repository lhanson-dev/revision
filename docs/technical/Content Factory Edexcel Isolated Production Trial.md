## What has actually been made executable

The first Market 1.2 prototype is an **isolated adapter projection of original material already present on main**, not newly generated by a paid Content Factory worker. `research/content-production-repeatability/edexcel-9bs0-market-content-preview.ts` reads the exact historical research authorship datasets and produces a normal `contentPackSchema` object with `manifest.status = 'planned'`. Its structure and operation are checked in `edexcel-9bs0-market-content-preview.test.ts`.

The isolated, provisional output contains:

- **23 Learn pages**: five cohesive original supply/equilibrium research lesson sections plus 18 short original demand/elasticity teaching specimens. These are NOT independently accepted complete A-level lessons; the 18 are particularly compact.
- **7 MCQ Practice items**, with authored correct options and explanations (not independently reviewed).
- **22 unscored DataDrills**, comprising four calculation exercises and 18 contextual-explanation exercises, with authored explanations/cautions.
- **0 approved scored written responses**. The corrected 10-mark original question `EXAM-ORIGINAL-02-V2` is explicitly recorded as a separate provisional candidate; the superseded earlier 8-mark and 10-mark questions are NOT emitted as live Exam Prep items.
- **0 published full exam papers**. No validated drawing assessment, approved holistic marking rubric or test learner mastery writes.

This is **format/adapter compatibility evidence**, not actual qualified content production and not a claim of full 26-skill coverage. Eight initial skills were inventoried in the earlier supply/equilibrium research, with one diagram-construction skill unassessed, and 18 further demand/elasticity authoring specimens remain unreviewed. The exact section denominator and adequacy have not been educationally approved.

The module sits under `research/`, not `content/**/index.ts`, so the Vite glob does not discover it as a learner catalogue pack. It has no effects on existing AQA Business/Psychology, routes, evidence writes or student sessions. `planned` is an additional fail-closed guard, not permission to register it in production. Existing generated Business content remains unchanged.

## Genuine end-to-end production HOLD

The proposal **does not** claim the existing provider workers have generated an Edexcel course. Before a paid section run:

1. Establish full, independently challenged **section-level** Course Truth and appropriate original Exam Truth for the exact cohort, not merely the 26 provisional identifiers.
2. Record operation-specific permission for every AI input. Pearson specification and support remain `REFERENCE_ONLY`: do not submit protected prose, mark schemes, cases, graphics or other substantial Pearson-owned text to a provider. The external OER rights for new AI operations remain unapproved.
3. Use the current original question's **holistic level-based** assessment logic only after exact AO/paper placement and independent answer reconstruction; do not reintroduce the withdrawn eight-mark prompt or superseded ten-mark points checklist.
4. Execute existing bounded workers with exact source/fingerprint checks, strict cost ceiling, per-call cost receipts, retries, elapsed time, human minutes, reusable remediation and artifact capture, **without overriding `ai_assured` or publication guards**.
5. Prove one complete lesson/practice/exam-question journey in an isolated application test, then a second section with no new React course-specific code. Only a separately assured `available` pack can enter the real learner catalogue.

The project currently lacks a prepared generic Edexcel production input with those qualifications; an AQA `workflow_dispatch` generator is AQA-7132-specific and gated to `main`. Reusing it by relabelling Edexcel inputs or fabricating the Foundation state would be invalid. A meaningful live Edexcel model run was therefore **not** attempted in this change.
