# Business course / assessment-component classification

**Date checked:** 2026-08-18  
**Purpose:** Current structural evidence for learner placement under `Course Content and Assessment Component Placement.md`.

This record does not replace or rewrite the historical content-accuracy assurance records for the existing packs. It records the later product-structure classification used to place already-assured learning content in the learner hierarchy.

## AQA A-level Business 7132

**Classification:** A — shared course learning.

Official AQA specification-at-a-glance:  
`https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/a-level/business-7132/specification/specification-at-a-glance`

Current AQA structure states that Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 each assess **all** A-level Business subject content. Their principal differences are assessment format and demand rather than ownership of separate syllabus topics.

Revision placement:

- ten subject areas presented once at AQA A-level Business course level;
- shared notes, flashcards, quick checks, quantitative/application practice and course/topic progress presented once;
- Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 presented inside Exam Prep;
- paper-specific written questions and simulations retain their paper identity.

## AQA AS Business 7131

**Classification:** A — shared course learning.

Official AQA specification-at-a-glance:  
`https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/as-level/business-7131/specification/specification-at-a-glance`

Current AQA structure states that both Paper 1 and Paper 2 assess **all** six AS Business subject-content areas. The papers therefore do not own separate topic syllabuses.

Revision placement:

- six subject areas presented once at AQA AS Business course level;
- shared learning/practice/progress not labelled as Paper 2 learning;
- paper-specific work presented inside Exam Prep.

**Current repository limitation:** only the assured AS Paper 2 content pack is currently published. The official existence of Paper 1 does not mean Revision has an assured Paper 1 pack. The learner catalogue must not imply otherwise.

## Revalidation trigger

Re-run this classification if AQA changes the specification/assessment structure, a replacement Business specification is adopted, or new component packs reveal genuinely component-specific learning content.
