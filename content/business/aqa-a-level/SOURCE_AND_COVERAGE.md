## 1. Course identity and currency

AQA identifies 7132 as its current **outgoing A-level Business specification** and instructs centres to continue using it for cohorts taking exams in **2027**. A newly accredited replacement is for first teaching from September 2026. Revision must therefore treat this course as valid for the 2027 exam cohort only unless later AQA evidence supports continued use.

This is a linear qualification. All three examinations are taken in the same series and all three may assess the full course content.

### Assessment objectives

- **AO1:** demonstrate knowledge of terms, concepts, theories, methods and models.
- **AO2:** apply knowledge and understanding to business contexts.
- **AO3:** analyse business issues and the effects of internal/external influences.
- **AO4:** evaluate quantitative and qualitative information, make informed judgements and propose evidence-based solutions.

Revision-authored AO allocations are internal design metadata and must not be presented as official AQA allocations for individual questions.

## 5. Cross-cutting expectations

The course must repeatedly require learners to:

- use varied business contexts: large/small, UK/global, service/manufacturing;
- connect functional decisions rather than learn ten isolated topics;
- interpret quantitative and non-quantitative evidence;
- consider uncertainty and the quality of data;
- consider technology, ethics, environmental effects and stakeholders;
- compare short- and long-term effects;
- make contextual, evidence-based judgements rather than generic lists;
- engage with strategic feasibility and risk.

## 6. Quantitative coverage target

Revision must deliberately teach and practise:

- percentages, percentage change, ratios, averages and fractions;
- index-number interpretation;
- market size/share/growth;
- cost, revenue, profit and break-even;
- contribution and margin of safety;
- productivity, unit cost and capacity utilisation;
- HR ratios;
- profitability margins;
- ROCE, current ratio, gearing, payables days, receivables days and inventory turnover;
- expected value and net gain;
- payback, average rate of return and net present value;
- interpretation (not calculation) of price and income elasticity;
- quantitative decision making in context.

Every stored numerical answer used in generated practice must be recomputed during the Content Accuracy Assurance Gate.

## 7. Content architecture decision

Use three coordinated packs:

- `business-aqa-a-level-7132-paper-1`
- `business-aqa-a-level-7132-paper-2`
- `business-aqa-a-level-7132-paper-3`

Shared course content will live under `content/business/aqa-a-level/shared/` **without an `index.ts`**, so it is not independently discovered by the catalogue. Each paper pack will import the same assured ten-area Learn/Practice layer and add its paper-specific simulator/metadata.

This matches the AQA rule that all three papers assess all course content while preserving Revision's paper/component hierarchy. The existing catalogue groups modules by exam board + qualification + specification code, so the three papers will appear as one AQA A-level Business 7132 course.

## 8. Known implementation limitation to assure

The current `examSchema` stores a single `caseHtml` per simulation. Paper 2 officially uses three separate data-response questions/contexts. Revision can preserve three distinct labelled stimuli within that one field, but this is a presentation approximation. It must be described as an **original AQA-aligned Paper 2 simulation**, not an exact digital reproduction of the official paper layout.

No shared schema or React change is required to add the course under this model.
