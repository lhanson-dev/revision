## What changed

### Guided case/application practice
The pack moves from **1** substantial guided case to **4**:

1. NorthPeak Bikes Ltd — existing product/manufacturing growth case;
2. Harbour Street Café — small owner-managed service business;
3. North City Care Services — labour-intensive service / decentralisation / technology case;
4. Atlas Outdoor plc — larger internationally exposed business with sustainability and supply-chain decisions.

The new contexts deliberately reduce overfitting to UK manufacturing cases and create transfer practice across ownership, scale, labour intensity, competition, technology, ethics/environment and international exposure.

### Full Paper 2 simulations
The pack moves from **1** full 90-minute / 80-mark original simulation to **3**:

1. Harbour Home Ltd — existing integrated manufacturing/growth case;
2. Lumen Hotels Ltd — service-sector case with labour, pricing, investment and finance decisions;
3. Orbit Fitness plc — national subscription-service case with segmentation, fixed costs, decentralisation and site strategy.

The two new simulations use approximately seven questions each and deliberately include calculation, application, analysis, evaluation and synoptic final responses. They are original Revision simulations and are not represented as official AQA past papers.

### Quantitative practice
The pack adds **10** new mixed-context data drills covering:

- market share;
- operating profit margin;
- employee costs as a percentage of turnover;
- labour turnover;
- expected value and net gain;
- capacity utilisation;
- cash-flow timing;
- break-even scenario change;
- labour productivity comparison;
- index-number interpretation and relative market position.

These supplement, rather than replace, the existing formulas, MCQs, data drills and calculations embedded in full simulations.
