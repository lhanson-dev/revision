## 5. Cross-cutting subject expectations

AQA expects students to consider business context, interrelationships, competition, ethical/environmental influences, uncertainty/data quality, technology, stakeholder effects and quantitative/non-quantitative information across the course.

Revision contains topic-link explanations and case/application material that deliberately connect functional areas. This is a strength of the current pack and fits Paper 2's synoptic case-study demands.

### Identified gap — index-number interpretation

The AQA subject-content introduction explicitly includes interpretation of index numbers in business decision making. The current simulator includes index-number data and asks the learner to calculate a percentage change from it, but the Learn content and shared formula/skills material do not currently teach **what an index number is or how to interpret one**.

**Classification:** material but narrow coverage gap.  
**Required remediation:** add a plain-English index-number learning point/example and corresponding active-recall or quick-check evidence before treating the pack as fully assured.

### Quantitative-skills annex caution

The annex also lists investment-appraisal outcomes among broader Business quantitative skills, while stating that skills not shown in bold are not part of the AS core unless specifically included by the board. The text extraction used for this audit does not preserve the annex's bold formatting reliably. The 7131 AS financial-content page requires the **concept of return on investment**, which the current pack covers, but does not explicitly require investment-appraisal calculations.

**Decision:** do not label investment-appraisal calculation as an AS gap without verification from an authoritative rendering/source that preserves the AS-core distinction.

## 6. Educational assurance verdict

### Strengths
- correct qualification/specification identity for the 2026 cohort;
- correct Paper 2 duration and raw marks;
- all six official AS subject areas represented;
- strong direct mapping between official sub-sections and current topic notes;
- quantitative formulas broadly align with required subject calculations;
- integrated case/application design is consistent with Paper 2's synoptic case-study nature;
- simulator is original content and is labelled as an original simulation rather than an official AQA paper.

### Gaps / limitations
1. **Index-number interpretation is not taught explicitly despite being used in the simulator and expected by AQA subject content.**
2. The simulator has eight questions while AQA describes Paper 2 as approximately seven; this is not inherently non-compliant, but exact current-paper format fidelity should be checked before marketing it as a close replica rather than an original full-paper simulation.
3. This retrospective audit verifies specification/topic alignment but is not an independent teacher/examiner review of every individual flashcard, generated question or marking-guidance sentence.
4. The AQA specification is outgoing after the 2026 cohort; this pack must not silently be reused for later cohorts if their specification changes.

## 7. Current decision

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS.**

The pack is substantially aligned with AQA AS Business 7131 and is suitable for the current pilot, but it should not be called fully assured until the explicit index-number learning gap is repaired and the changed content passes structural CI and a focused educational re-check.

The pack may remain `available` during this narrow remediation because the identified gap does not invalidate the majority of the current content and the pack is already in active pilot use. The remediation should be completed before this pack becomes the benchmark for new-pack completeness.
