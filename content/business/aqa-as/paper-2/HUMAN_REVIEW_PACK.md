## 1. Reviewer brief

Revision is a personalised exam-revision product. This pack is original Revision-authored learning and practice content built to align with AQA AS Business 7131. It is **not official AQA content** unless an item is explicitly identified as such.

Your job is not primarily to improve writing style. Your job is to identify anything that could teach a learner the wrong fact, rule, method, exam behaviour or curriculum priority.

Please review the material against the exact 7131 specification and official AQA assessment material linked below. Treat official AQA material as authority where it conflicts with Revision wording.

## 6. Human review questions

Please answer these questions for the pack as a whole and record item-level findings where necessary.

### Curriculum and factual accuracy
- Does every substantial learner-facing statement accurately reflect AQA AS Business 7131?
- Is any content outside scope in a way that could distort learner priorities?
- Are definitions, models, formulas and relationships stated with appropriate conditions/limitations?
- Are there important specification requirements that appear missing or materially under-taught?

### Plain-English teaching quality
- Does simplification preserve the underlying Business meaning?
- Could any explanation cause a learner to memorise an incorrect absolute rule?
- Are common misconceptions likely to be created or reinforced?
- Are examples sufficiently realistic and correctly interpreted?

### Retrieval and quick checks
- Are flashcard answers accurate and sufficiently precise?
- Do MCQs have one defensible best answer?
- Are distractors plausible without being misleading?
- Do explanations teach the right reason, not merely the right option?

### Quantitative content
- Are all formulas appropriate for this specification?
- Are every calculation, unit, percentage, ratio and index interpretation correct?
- Are worked answers expressed in a way AQA learners should understand?
- Are any quantitative techniques incorrectly prioritised or omitted?

### Cases and exam practice
- Are invented business scenarios internally coherent and educationally plausible?
- Do questions test concepts that are actually in scope?
- Is the level of application/analysis/evaluation appropriate for AS?
- Are command words and marks broadly consistent with AQA practice?
- Are simulations recognisably Paper-2-like without falsely claiming to reproduce an official paper?

### Marking guidance and exam technique
- Would following the guidance improve a student's AQA answer rather than teach a fake formulaic rule?
- Does guidance reward sound contextual analysis/evaluation?
- Are any rules too rigid or inconsistent with how AQA marks responses?
- Is the guidance clearly Revision-authored rather than represented as an official mark scheme?

### Completeness and balance
- Is the coverage balanced enough that a learner would not be falsely reassured after studying only this pack?
- Are some areas materially overrepresented/underrepresented relative to likely assessment needs?
- Are three full simulations and four guided cases sufficiently varied for a strong pilot benchmark?

## 8. Severity and final decision

### Severity
- **Blocking** — likely to materially teach the wrong fact, method, curriculum requirement or exam behaviour; learner-facing use should stop until fixed.
- **Material** — significant error, omission, scope drift or assessment problem that must be corrected before benchmark approval.
- **Minor** — low-risk precision/wording problem that does not materially misteach.
- **No issue** — checked and acceptable.

### Final decision
- **PASS** — no unresolved blocking/material findings; pack is factually sound, in scope and educationally suitable for AQA AS Business 7131 at the reviewed version.
- **CONDITIONAL PASS** — suitable with explicit non-critical limitations and defined remediation/revalidation.
- **FAIL / HOLD** — unresolved blocking/material findings, review scope insufficient, or source/curriculum uncertainty prevents approval.
